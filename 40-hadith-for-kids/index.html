About the Course

What Is the 40 Hadith for Kids Course?

For centuries, Muslim children have grown up memorizing forty hadith. Our course follows that tradition with short, authentic hadith chosen for young learners, many from Imam an-Nawawi's famous Forty Hadith collection.

Children learn the Arabic, its meaning and how to act on it at home and at school. For example, the first hadith teaches that actions are judged by intentions (Sahih al-Bukhari 1), and another that none of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself (Sahih al-Bukhari 13, Sahih Muslim 45).

The course sits alongside our Islamic studies classes and online Quran classes for kids.

Questions and Answers

40 Hadith for Kids: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do 40 Hadith classes cost?

40 Hadith for Kids fees are monthly, per student: 30 minute classes are $15 for 2 days, $25 for 4 days, $30 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are $20 for 2 days, $30 for 4 days, $35 for 5 days a week. In Pakistan, 30 minute classes are Rs 3,500 for 2 days, Rs 6,000 for 4 days, Rs 7,500 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are Rs 4,500 for 2 days, Rs 7,500 for 4 days, Rs 9,500 for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

What is the 40 Hadith for kids course?

It is a course where children memorize and understand short, authentic hadith, many from Imam an-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, with the Arabic, the meaning in English or Urdu and a lesson for daily life.

What age is the 40 Hadith course for?

It suits children from around age 6 to teenagers. Younger children learn shorter hadith with more stories and repetition.

Are the hadith authentic?

Yes. We only teach authentic hadith, each with its source, for example Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim.

Will my child memorize the hadith in Arabic?

Yes. Children memorize each hadith in Arabic, with regular revision, and learn its meaning so they understand what they say.

Can hadith be combined with Quran classes?

Yes. Many parents add a few minutes of hadith to each Quran class, or study it on its own plan.

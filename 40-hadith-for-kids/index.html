About the Course

What Is the 40 Hadith for Kids Course?

For centuries, Muslim children have grown up memorizing forty hadith. Our course follows that tradition with short, authentic hadith chosen for young learners, many from Imam an-Nawawi's famous Forty Hadith collection.

Children learn the Arabic, its meaning and how to act on it at home and at school. For example, the first hadith teaches that actions are judged by intentions (Sahih al-Bukhari 1), and another that none of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself (Sahih al-Bukhari 13, Sahih Muslim 45).

The course sits alongside our Islamic studies classes and online Quran classes for kids.

Questions and Answers

40 Hadith for Kids: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do 40 Hadith classes cost?

Fees depend on how many days a week you study and how long each class is. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share them with you. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

What is the 40 Hadith for kids course?

It is a course where children memorize and understand short, authentic hadith, many from Imam an-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, with the Arabic, the meaning in English, Urdu or Arabic and a lesson for daily life. Each hadith is linked to a real-life situation, like kindness, honesty or intention. Pair it with our Islamic stories for kids at bedtime.

What age is the 40 Hadith course for?

It suits children from around age 6 to teenagers. Younger children learn shorter hadith with more stories and repetition. Younger children learn shorter hadith with simple stories, while older children learn longer ones with more explanation. The teacher adjusts the pace to each child after the free trial.

Are the hadith authentic?

Yes. We only teach authentic hadith, each with its source, for example Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim. Imam an-Nawawi chose hadith that scholars have accepted, and each lesson gives the source, such as Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim, so families can look it up.

Will my child memorize the hadith in Arabic?

Yes. Children memorize each hadith in Arabic, with regular revision, and learn its meaning so they understand what they say. Children usually memorise the short Arabic text with its meaning, and longer hadith by their key words. The goal is understanding and practice, not only memorisation.

Can hadith be combined with Quran classes?

Yes. Many parents add a few minutes of hadith to each Quran class, or study it on its own plan. Many families add one hadith at the end of each Quran class. See our Islamic studies online and online Quran classes for kids.

Which hadith are in the course?

The course follows Imam an-Nawawi's Forty Hadith, which includes the hadith of intention, the hadith of Jibreel on Islam, iman and ihsan, and hadith on kindness, honesty, good manners and avoiding harm. Teachers choose the order and depth to suit the child's age.

How long does the 40 Hadith course take?

Most children learn one hadith every week or two, so the course usually takes several months to a year, depending on age and class frequency. Younger children may learn the shorter hadith first and return to the longer ones later. Revision is part of every class.

How does this course help my child's character?

Each hadith is linked to everyday life: telling the truth, being kind to parents, sharing, controlling anger and having a good intention. Children are encouraged to practise one lesson each week. Many parents pair it with our daily duas course and salah classes.

How do I book the 40 Hadith course?

Fill in the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp with your child's age and level. We reply within 24 hours and arrange 2 free trial classes with a female teacher. After the trial, fees are paid monthly in advance, and families with more than one child get a discount.

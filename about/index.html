Our Story and Promise

About Quran Online Tarteel

Quran Online Tarteel is an online Quran academy for children and women, taught only by female teachers. Our name comes from tarteel, reciting the Quran slowly and beautifully, and that is what we help every student do.

Our Story

A Note from Our Founder, a Mother

“

Assalamu alaikum,

I began teaching the Quran in 2010, one child at a time. As a mother, I know the questions that sit in a parent’s heart before the first lesson: Will the teacher be patient with my child? Will my daughter feel safe? Will my son really learn, or just sit in front of a screen?

Those questions shaped everything we do. Every teacher here is a woman with more than ten years of teaching, and before she teaches a single child we listen to her recitation and watch how she teaches. Parents can sit in on any lesson, and the class link always comes to your phone, not your child’s.

Since then, more than 500 children around the world have learned with us. Some started with the Arabic alphabet and now read the Quran by themselves. And it is never too late: mothers learn with us too, gently and at their own pace. Nothing makes me happier.

Mera khwab bas itna hai ke har bacha Quran ko sahi bhi parhe aur pyaar se bhi.

(My only dream is that every child reads the Quran correctly, and with love.)

With duas for your family,Founder, Quran Online Tarteel

Our Mission

Our Mission: Quran Learning That Feels Safe and Personal

Many families want their daughters, sons and mothers to learn the Quran properly, but cannot find a female teacher nearby, or a class time that fits. We built Quran Online Tarteel to solve that, with live one-to-one classes from home, in any time zone.

Allah says in the Quran: وَرَتِّلِ ٱلْقُرْءَانَ تَرْتِيلًا, "and recite the Quran with measured recitation" (Al-Muzzammil 73:4). That verse gives our academy its name and its purpose.

Safety and Trust

Our Promise to Every Parent

Simple rules we keep in every class, so you can relax while your child learns.

Your child learns with a woman

Every teacher is a woman. Her camera stays off in class, and if you want to be sure, she will turn it on once so you can see for yourself.

You are always welcome

Sit in on any lesson, any day. You never need to ask first.

The link comes to you

Class links and messages go to the parent’s phone number, never straight to your child.

Children stay in view

Young children keep their camera on, so the teacher can see that they are following and focused.

No recordings, ever

Lessons are never recorded, by us or by anyone else. What happens in class stays private.

The same teacher every time

Your child keeps the same teacher week after week, so trust and progress build together. Want a change? Just tell us.

10 Benefits of Reciting the Quran, from the Quran and Authentic Hadith

Ten virtues of reciting the Quran from the Quran and authentic hadith, with references, and simple ways to make daily recitation a habit.

Reciting the Quran is one of the most rewarding acts of worship a Muslim can do, and its benefits begin with the very first letter. The Quran and authentic hadith describe rewards in this life and the next: multiplied good deeds, peace in the heart, a high rank for its companions and intercession on the Day of Judgement. Here are ten of those benefits, each with its source, and simple ways to make daily recitation a habit.

Key takeaways
  • Every letter recited earns a good deed, multiplied by ten (At-Tirmidhi 2910).
  • The Quran will intercede for its companions (Sahih Muslim 804).
  • The one who struggles with recitation gets a double reward (Sahih al-Bukhari 4937, Sahih Muslim 798).
  • The best of people are those who learn the Quran and teach it (Sahih al-Bukhari 5027).

Rewards for Every Letter and Every Effort

The first group of benefits is about reward. They show how generous Allah is with those who open His Book.

1. Ten good deeds for every letter

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allah will have a good deed, and a good deed is multiplied by ten. I do not say Alif Lam Mim is a letter, but Alif is a letter, Lam is a letter and Mim is a letter" (At-Tirmidhi 2910). A single short surah earns hundreds of good deeds.

2. A double reward for those who find it hard

This hadith is a gift to every beginner, every child in the Noorani Qaida and every adult learning the Quran later in life. Struggling is not failure. It is double reward.

3. The best of people

"The best of you are those who learn the Quran and teach it" (Sahih al-Bukhari 5027). Learning and teaching the Quran raises a person above others in the sight of Allah.

Benefits in the Hereafter

Some of the greatest benefits of the Quran are waiting for us after this life.

4. Intercession on the Day of Judgement

"Read the Quran, for it will come on the Day of Resurrection as an intercessor for its companions" (Sahih Muslim 804). Surah Al-Baqarah and Aal Imran will come like two clouds shading their companions. Surah Al-Mulk intercedes for its reader until he is forgiven (At-Tirmidhi 2891).

5. A rank as high as your last verse

"It will be said to the companion of the Quran: Read, rise and recite as you used to recite in the world, for your rank will be at the last verse you recite" (Abu Dawud 1464, At-Tirmidhi 2914). This is one reason families value Hifz.

Benefits for the Heart and the Home

The Quran also changes the life of the one who recites it, here and now.

6. Peace and tranquillity

"No people gather in one of the houses of Allah, reciting the Book of Allah and studying it among themselves, except that tranquillity descends upon them, mercy covers them, the angels surround them and Allah mentions them to those with Him" (Sahih Muslim 2699).

7. Healing and guidance

8. Protection for the home

"Do not make your houses into graves. Shaytan flees from the house in which Surah Al-Baqarah is recited" (Sahih Muslim 780). Reciting Ayatul Kursi and the Quls at night is also protection.

9. A beautiful character

The Prophet, peace be upon him, compared the believer who recites the Quran to a citron: its fragrance is pleasant and its taste is good (Sahih al-Bukhari 5020). The Quran shapes the character of the one who lives with it.

10. Remembrance that raises you

Allah calls the Quran adh-Dhikr, the Reminder. Reciting it is the greatest form of dhikr, and Allah remembers those who remember Him.

Understanding multiplies the benefit

Allah asks: "Do they not reflect upon the Quran?" (Quran 47:24). Reading with meaning deepens every benefit. Our Translation and Tafseer classes and the guide to Tafseer for beginners help.

Share them with your family

Read one benefit aloud at dinner or bedtime and talk about it. Children who know why the Quran matters are more eager to learn it. Our online Quran classes for kids build on that motivation.

How to Make Daily Recitation a Habit

Knowing the benefits is the first step. Keeping a daily habit is the second.

Start small and fixed

Five minutes after Fajr or before bed, every day, is better than an hour once a week. Tie it to a prayer so it becomes automatic.

Improve your recitation

If you are unsure of your reading, learn with a teacher. Our online Quran classes for beginners and online Tajweed classes help you recite with confidence. For a bigger goal, try our Ramadan Quran plan.

Questions and Answers

Benefits of the Quran: Questions Answered

What are the benefits of reciting the Quran?

Reciting the Quran earns ten good deeds for every letter, brings tranquillity and mercy, and the Quran will intercede for its companions on the Day of Judgement, as authentic hadith report. It is a healing and guidance for believers, protects the home from Shaytan and shapes good character. Even those who struggle to read it receive a double reward.

What is the reward for reading the Quran?

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said whoever reads one letter of the Quran gets a good deed multiplied by ten, as narrated by At-Tirmidhi. He explained that Alif Lam Mim is three letters, not one. On top of that, those who read with difficulty get a double reward, and the Quran will intercede for its regular readers in the Hereafter.

Is there reward for reading the Quran without understanding?

Yes. Scholars agree there is reward for reciting the Quran even without understanding its meaning, because the hadith of ten good deeds per letter is general. Still, the Quran asks us to reflect on its verses, so try to learn the meaning gradually through translation and Tafseer. Understanding increases both the reward and the effect on your heart.

How much Quran should I read daily?

There is no fixed minimum. Start with an amount you can keep every day, even a page or a few verses. Many people aim for one juz a day in Ramadan to finish the Quran in a month, and a few pages a day the rest of the year. Consistency matters more than quantity, because small regular deeds are most beloved to Allah.

Can I read the Quran on my phone?

Yes, you can read the Quran from a phone or tablet and earn the reward of recitation. Many scholars say you do not need wudu to read from a screen, though it is better to be in wudu. Use a reliable app with a clear Uthmani script. For learning correct pronunciation, combine reading with a live teacher.

Surah Al-Mulk: Benefits, Meaning and How to Memorize It

What authentic hadith say about Surah Al-Mulk, its main themes, the Sunnah of reciting it at night and a two week plan to memorise all 30 verses.

Surah Al-Mulk (The Sovereignty) is the 67th surah of the Quran, with 30 verses, and opens the 29th juz. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said it intercedes for its reader until he is forgiven, and he would not sleep until he had recited it. Many Muslims recite it every night before sleeping. This guide explains the authentic benefits of Surah Al-Mulk, its meaning and themes, and a simple plan to memorise it.

Key takeaways
  • 30 verses, a Makki surah, the first surah of Juz 29 (Tabarak).
  • It intercedes for its reader until he is forgiven (At-Tirmidhi 2891, Abu Dawud 1400).
  • The Prophet, peace be upon him, did not sleep until he recited As-Sajdah and Al-Mulk (At-Tirmidhi 2892).
  • Recited every night, it is described as protection from the punishment of the grave.

The Benefits of Surah Al-Mulk in Authentic Hadith

The virtues of Surah Al-Mulk come from hadith that scholars have graded reliable.

It intercedes until its reader is forgiven

Protection from the punishment of the grave

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said that whoever recites Tabarak alladhi biyadihil mulk every night, Allah will protect him by it from the punishment of the grave, and that it was called al-mani'ah, the protector (An-Nasa'i in As-Sunan al-Kubra, graded hasan by Al-Albani).

The Prophet's nightly practice

Jabir reported that the Prophet, peace be upon him, would not sleep until he recited Alif Lam Mim Tanzil (Surah As-Sajdah) and Tabarak alladhi biyadihil mulk (At-Tirmidhi 2892). This is the basis for reciting Al-Mulk at night. Add it to your duas before sleeping.

What Surah Al-Mulk Is About

The surah begins by declaring Allah's complete control over everything:

Main themes

Verses Theme
1 to 5 Allah's sovereignty, life and death as a test, the perfect creation of the heavens
6 to 11 The regret of those who denied the warning
12 to 14 Reward for those who fear Allah unseen; Allah knows what is in hearts
15 to 22 The earth made easy for us, birds held in the sky, provision from Allah alone
23 to 30 Hearing, sight and hearts as gifts; who could bring water if it sank away?

Lessons for daily life

Life is a test of who does the best deeds, not the most. Everything we rely on, from the ground we walk on to the water we drink, is held by Allah. The surah ends with a question that leaves the reader humble.

Learn it with meaning

Understanding Al-Mulk makes the nightly recitation powerful. Our Translation and Tafseer classes cover it word by word, and Tafseer for beginners shows how to start.

When and How to Recite Surah Al-Mulk

There is no fixed time required, but the Sunnah points to the night.

Every night before sleep

Following the hadith of Jabir, recite it after Isha or in bed. Many families make it part of the bedtime routine with their children.

Recite with Tajweed

Al-Mulk has many examples of madd, ghunnah and qalqalah, which makes it a good practice surah. See what Tajweed is or improve with our online Tajweed classes.

Listen if you cannot read yet

If you are still learning to read, listen to it nightly with a translation while you learn. Listening is the first step to memorising.

How to Memorize Surah Al-Mulk in Two Weeks

Thirty verses sounds like a lot, but split into small portions it takes two to three weeks.

Days 1 to 3: verses 1 to 6. Listen ten times, then repeat each verse aloud until fluent.

Days 4 to 6: verses 7 to 12, then recite 1 to 12 together.

Days 7 to 9: verses 13 to 19, then recite 1 to 19.

Days 10 to 12: verses 20 to 25, then recite 1 to 25.

Days 13 to 14: verses 26 to 30, then the whole surah, twice a day.

Ongoing: recite it every night. That nightly recitation is your revision.

Tips that help

Use the same mushaf every day, recite new verses in your sunnah prayers and recite to a teacher before moving on. Read how to memorise the Quran for more techniques.

For children

Children can memorise Al-Mulk after finishing much of Juz Amma, often at 3 to 5 verses a week. Our online Hifz program plans it with them.

Questions and Answers

Surah Al-Mulk: Questions Answered

What are the benefits of Surah Al-Mulk?

Authentic hadith report that Surah Al-Mulk, a surah of thirty verses, interceded for a man until he was forgiven, as narrated by At-Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud. Ibn Mas'ud described it as protection from the punishment of the grave for whoever recites it every night. The Prophet, peace be upon him, would not sleep until he recited it.

When should I read Surah Al-Mulk?

The Sunnah is to recite Surah Al-Mulk at night, before sleeping, because Jabir reported that the Prophet, peace be upon him, would not sleep until he had recited it and Surah As-Sajdah. You can recite it after Isha or in bed. Reciting it at any other time is also rewarded, like any part of the Quran.

Does Surah Al-Mulk protect from the punishment of the grave?

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said that whoever recites Surah Al-Mulk every night will be protected by Allah from the punishment of the grave, and that it was called the protector. This narration is in An-Nasa'i's As-Sunan al-Kubra and was graded hasan by Al-Albani. It encourages Muslims to make it a regular nightly recitation.

How many verses are in Surah Al-Mulk?

Surah Al-Mulk has 30 verses. It is the 67th surah of the Quran and the first surah of the 29th juz, which is often called Juz Tabarak after its opening word. It is a Makki surah, revealed in Makkah, and it focuses on Allah's sovereignty, creation, the purpose of life and the Hereafter.

How long does it take to memorize Surah Al-Mulk?

Most adults can memorise Surah Al-Mulk in about two to three weeks by learning five or six verses every three days and revising daily. Children usually take longer, often one to two months at a few verses a week. Reciting it every night after memorising keeps it strong, which is also the Sunnah practice.

Questions Before You Contact Us

How fast will you reply to my message?

We reply within 24 hours, usually much sooner on WhatsApp. If you message late at night in your time zone, expect an answer the next morning.

Can I book my free trial classes through this page?

Yes. Choose "Book free trial classes" in the form or message us on WhatsApp. We will arrange 2 free live classes with a female teacher.

Can I ask about fees before joining?

Yes. Fees depend on days per week and class length. Ask us on WhatsApp or by email, or see our fees and plans page.

Can I speak with the teacher before classes start?

Yes. Your first free class is your chance to meet your female teacher and ask questions before you decide.

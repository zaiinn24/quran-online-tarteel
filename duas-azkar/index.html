Duas & Azkar: Questions Answered

Are these duas authentic?

Yes. Every dua is from the Quran or from authentic hadith, with its reference. The Arabic is taken from Hisn al-Muslim and the King Fahd Complex Quran text.

Can my child learn these duas with a teacher?

Yes. Our female teachers teach duas with correct pronunciation and meaning in the online duas course, with 2 free classes.

Are the translations in Urdu?

Each dua has its meaning in English and in Roman Urdu (Urdu written in English letters), so the whole family can understand it.

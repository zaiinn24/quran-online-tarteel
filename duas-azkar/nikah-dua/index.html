Blessings for a New Family

Nikah Dua for the Bride and Groom

The Sunnah dua for a newly married couple, Barakallahu lak wa baraka alayk, the groom's dua and the Quranic dua for a happy family, with meaning in English and Roman Urdu.

When a couple marries, the Sunnah is to pray for blessings in their marriage. Here is the dua to say to the newlyweds, the dua the groom says for his bride, and a dua from the Quran for a happy, righteous family.

To congratulate the newly married couple

بارَكَ اللّهُ لَك، وَبارَكَ عَلَيْك، وَجَمَعَ بَيْنَكُما في خَيْر

TransliterationBārakallāhu lak, wa bāraka `alayk, wa jama`a baynakumā fī khayr.

EnglishMay Allah bless you, shower His blessings upon you, and bring you together in goodness.

Roman UrduAllah tumhare liye barkat de, tum par barkat naazil kare aur tum dono ko bhalai mein ikattha rakhe.

Reference: Abu Dawud; At-Tirmidhi; Ibn Majah (Hisn al-Muslim 190)

The groom's dua for his bride

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَهَا، وَخَيْرَ مَا جَبَلْتَهَا عَلَيْهِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا، وَشَرِّ مَا جَبَلْتَهَا عَلَيْهِ

TransliterationAllāhumma innī as'aluka khayrahā wa khayra mā jabaltahā `alayh, wa a`ūdhu bika min sharrihā wa sharri mā jabaltahā `alayh.

EnglishO Allah, I ask You for her goodness and the goodness of the nature You have given her, and I seek Your protection from any harm in her and in the nature You have given her.

Roman UrduAe Allah, main Tujh se is ki bhalai aur us fitrat ki bhalai mangta hoon jis par Tu ne ise paida kiya, aur is ki buraai aur us fitrat ki buraai se Teri panah mangta hoon jis par Tu ne ise paida kiya.

Reference: Abu Dawud; Ibn Majah (Hisn al-Muslim 191)

For a happy, righteous family

رَبَّنَا هَبۡ لَنَا مِنۡ أَزۡوَٰجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّـٰتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعۡيُنٖ وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا (٧٤)

TransliterationRabbanā hab lanā min azwājinā wa dhurriyyātinā qurrata a`yunin waj`alnā lil-muttaqīna imāmā.

EnglishOur Lord, grant us from our spouses and our children the coolness of our eyes, and make us leaders for the righteous.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, hamein hamare jeewan saathiyon aur aulad se aankhon ki thandak ata farma, aur hamein parhezgaron ka imam bana.

Reference: Quran 25:74

Nikah Dua for the Bride and Groom: Questions Answered

What is the dua for a newly married couple?

"Barakallahu lak, wa baraka 'alayk, wa jama'a baynakuma fi khayr" - May Allah bless you, shower blessings upon you and unite you in goodness.

Is "Barakallahu lakuma" correct too?

Yes. When speaking to both the bride and groom together, many people say "Barakallahu lakuma". The wording above is the form reported in the hadith.

Can women learn these duas with a teacher?

Yes. Our female teachers teach duas with meaning in the online duas course.

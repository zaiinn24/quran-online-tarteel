Duas Allah Taught Us

40 Rabbana Duas from the Quran

All 40 Rabbana duas from the Quran in Arabic (Uthmani text) with transliteration, meaning in English and Roman Urdu, and the surah and ayah, plus the Rabbi duas of the Prophets.

Many of the most beautiful duas are in the Quran itself: the words of the Prophets and the believers, many of them beginning with Rabbana, "Our Lord". Here they are with the Arabic in the Uthmani script, an easy transliteration, their meaning in English and Roman Urdu, and the surah and ayah so you can find them in your Mushaf.

2:127 · Accept from us

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلۡ مِنَّآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ (١٢٧)

TransliterationRabbanā taqabbal minnā innaka antas-samī`ul-`alīm.

EnglishOur Lord, accept this from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, ham se qabool farma, beshak Tu sab kuch sunne wala, jaanne wala hai.

Reference: Quran 2:127

2:128 · Make us Muslims to You

رَبَّنَا وَٱجۡعَلۡنَا مُسۡلِمَيۡنِ لَكَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِنَآ أُمَّةࣰ مُّسۡلِمَةࣰ لَّكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبۡ عَلَيۡنَآۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ (١٢٨)

TransliterationRabbanā waj`alnā muslimayni laka wa min dhurriyyatinā ummatan muslimatan lak, wa arinā manāsikanā wa tub `alaynā, innaka antat-tawwābur-raḥīm.

EnglishOur Lord, make us submissive to You and make from our descendants a nation submissive to You. Show us our ways of worship and accept our repentance. You are the Accepter of repentance, the Merciful.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, hamein apna farmanbardar bana aur hamari aulad mein se bhi ek farmanbardar ummat bana, hamein ibadat ke tareeqe dikha aur hamari tauba qabool farma, beshak Tu tauba qabool karne wala, reham karne wala hai.

Reference: Quran 2:128

2:201 · Good in this world and the next

رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنۡيَا حَسَنَةࣰ وَفِي ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ حَسَنَةࣰ وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ (٢٠١)

TransliterationRabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā `adhāban-nār.

EnglishOur Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, hamein duniya mein bhi bhalai de aur aakhirat mein bhi bhalai de, aur hamein aag ke azaab se bacha.

Reference: Quran 2:201

2:250 · Patience and firm feet

رَبَّنَآ أَفۡرِغۡ عَلَيۡنَا صَبۡرࣰا وَثَبِّتۡ أَقۡدَامَنَا وَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ (٢٥٠)

TransliterationRabbanā afrigh `alaynā ṣabran wa thabbit aqdāmanā wanṣurnā `alal-qawmil-kāfirīn.

EnglishOur Lord, pour patience upon us, make our feet firm and help us against the disbelieving people.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, ham par sabr undel de, hamare qadam jama de aur kafir qaum ke muqable mein hamari madad farma.

Reference: Quran 2:250

2:286 · Do not place a heavy burden on us

رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحۡمِلۡ عَلَيۡنَآ إِصۡرࣰا كَمَا حَمَلۡتَهُۥ عَلَى ٱلَّذِينَ مِن قَبۡلِنَاۚ

TransliterationRabbanā wa lā taḥmil `alaynā iṣran kamā ḥamaltahū `alal-ladhīna min qablinā.

EnglishOur Lord, do not place on us a burden like the one You placed on those before us.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, ham par aisa bojh na daal jaisa Tu ne ham se pehle logon par daala.

Reference: Quran 2:286

2:286 · Do not burden us beyond our strength

رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلۡنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِۦۖ وَٱعۡفُ عَنَّا وَٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَٱرۡحَمۡنَآۚ أَنتَ مَوۡلَىٰنَا فَٱنصُرۡنَا عَلَى ٱلۡقَوۡمِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ (٢٨٦)

TransliterationRabbanā wa lā tuḥammilnā mā lā ṭāqata lanā bih, wa`fu `annā waghfir lanā warḥamnā, anta mawlānā fanṣurnā `alal-qawmil-kāfirīn.

EnglishOur Lord, do not burden us with more than we can bear. Pardon us, forgive us and have mercy on us. You are our Protector, so help us against the disbelieving people.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, ham par itna bojh na daal jis ki hamein taaqat nahin. Hamein maaf farma, bakhsh de aur ham par reham farma. Tu hi hamara Maula hai, pas kafir qaum ke muqable mein hamari madad farma.

Reference: Quran 2:286

3:8 · Keep our hearts steady

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغۡ قُلُوبَنَا بَعۡدَ إِذۡ هَدَيۡتَنَا وَهَبۡ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحۡمَةًۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلۡوَهَّابُ (٨)

TransliterationRabbanā lā tuzigh qulūbanā ba`da idh hadaytanā wa hab lanā min ladunka raḥmah, innaka antal-wahhāb.

Roman UrduAe hamare Rab, hidayat dene ke baad hamare dilon ko tedha na kar, aur hamein apne paas se rehmat ata farma. Beshak Tu hi bahut dene wala hai.

Reference: Quran 3:8

3:192 · Save us from the Fire

3:193 · We heard the caller to faith

Questions and Answers

40 Rabbana Duas from the Quran: Questions Answered

What does Rabbana mean?

Rabbana means "Our Lord". Many duas in the Quran begin with it, and Rabbi means "My Lord".

Can I read Rabbana duas in salah?

Many scholars allow reciting Quranic duas such as Rabbana atina in the last sitting before salam. Ask your teacher about the practice your family follows.

Which Rabbana dua is the most famous?

Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah (Quran 2:201) is the dua the Prophet, peace be upon him, made most often, as reported in Sahih al-Bukhari.

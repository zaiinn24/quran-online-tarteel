Fees: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do online Quran classes cost?

Fees depend on how many days a week you study and how long each class is. Message us on WhatsApp or fill in the form and we will send the current monthly fees for the plan that fits you.

Are there free classes before paying?

Yes. Every new student gets 3 free trial classes with no card needed.

Is there a discount for siblings?

Ask us on WhatsApp about family plans when more than one child or a mother and child study together.

Do I pay per class or per month?

Plans are monthly, based on the number of classes per week you choose.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to more or fewer days a week from the next month.

  6. Are class times available in your time zone?

Questions and Answers

Female Quran Teachers: Frequently Asked Questions

Are all your Quran teachers female?

Yes. Every teacher at Quran Online Tarteel is a woman, and she teaches both girls and boys under 18 as well as adult women. They teach children under 18, both boys and girls, and adult women. Read our guide on how to choose a female Quran teacher online to know what to look for.

How are your female Quran teachers checked?

Before a teacher takes a class, we listen to her recitation, check her Tajweed and makharij, and see how she teaches children and adults. We also look at her English, patience and punctuality. Every teacher has 10 or more years of teaching experience, and her recitation and teaching are checked by the academy before she teaches. We also listen to feedback from families after the trial and throughout the year.

Will I have the same teacher every class?

Yes. You keep the same teacher so she remembers your level, the mistakes you have fixed and what comes next. If you ever want to change, just tell us. Staying with one teacher means she knows exactly which letters, rules or pages you find hard. If you ever want a different teacher, we arrange one within 2 to 3 days.

Can I choose a teacher who speaks Urdu or Arabic too?

All teachers explain in English. Tell us in the form if you prefer a teacher who also speaks Urdu or Arabic and we will try to match you. Tell us your preferred language when you book. Our teachers explain in English, Urdu or Arabic, and the Quran is always read in Arabic. Children raised abroad usually prefer English, while many mothers choose Urdu.

Can I meet the teacher before paying?

Yes. Your first 2 classes are free, so you get to know your teacher and see how she teaches before you decide. The 2 free trial classes are with the teacher who will continue with you, so you can judge her patience, corrections and timing first. After the trial, fees are paid monthly in advance.

Which courses do your female teachers teach?

Our female teachers teach Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, translation and Tafseer, Islamic studies, salah and daily duas. Each teacher focuses on the courses she knows best, and we match you with the right one after the free trial, based on age, level and language.

Do your teachers teach students in my country?

Yes. We teach families in many countries, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Classes are booked in your local time, and each country page lists the usual slots and fees in your currency.

How do I book a female Quran teacher?

Fill in the short form on this page or message us on WhatsApp with the student's age, level, preferred language and time. We reply within 24 hours and arrange 2 free trial classes with a female teacher. If you are happy, you continue with the same teacher, and fees are paid monthly in advance after the trial.

Do teachers give updates to parents?

Yes. Teachers send parents regular updates on WhatsApp about what the child learned, what to revise and how they are doing. Parents can also sit in on any lesson. We do not run formal tests or certificates, but you always know exactly where your child is and what comes next.

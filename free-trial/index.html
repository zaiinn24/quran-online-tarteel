Questions and Answers

Free Online Quran Classes: Questions Answered

Are the 2 trial classes really free?

Yes. The first 2 classes are completely free. We do not ask for card or payment details to book them.

Do I have to continue after the free classes?

No. After the 2 classes you decide. If you continue, you choose a monthly plan. If not, there is nothing to cancel.

Who can book the free trial?

Children under 18 (boys and girls) and adult women. Adult men are not enrolled because every class is taught by a female teacher.

How soon can the first free class happen?

We message you on WhatsApp within 24 hours of your request. Most families have their first class within one to two days.

Can I book free classes for more than one child?

Yes. Select "My children and me" or mention it in your message and we will arrange a free trial for each child.

Which course can I try for free?

Any course: Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Translation and Tafseer or Islamic studies. If you are not sure, the teacher will recommend one.

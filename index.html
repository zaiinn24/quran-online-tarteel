About Quran Online Tarteel

Learn the Quran Online From the Comfort of Home

Quran Online Tarteel provides online Quran and Islamic education for families and women seeking a structured way to learn from home. Our learning approach is centred on the learner's goals, current level and schedule.

Female teachers deliver the online lessons. Families can use the free trial classes to discuss their learning needs before choosing a suitable program.

Frequently Asked Questions

Online Quran Classes: Common Questions

Review the basic details before sending your enquiry.

Are Quran Online Tarteel classes conducted online?

Yes. The academy provides online classes, allowing learners to study from home.

Do you provide female Quran teachers?

Yes. The academy's classes are delivered by female teachers.

Can children join online Quran classes?

Yes. Boys and girls under 18 can enquire about suitable classes. A parent or guardian should manage the enquiry.

Can adult women learn the Quran online?

Yes. Adult women can enquire about Quran reading, Tajweed and other available learning programs.

Can adult men enrol in classes?

No. Adult learners aged 18 and above are eligible only if they are women. Children under 18 may be boys or girls.

How many free trial classes are available?

Three free trial classes are available so you can explore the learning experience before choosing a package.

Which programs can I enquire about?

You can enquire about Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Tafseer, Quran translation and suitable Islamic studies topics.

How much do online Quran classes cost?

Fees depend on the package and class arrangement. Contact the academy to confirm current prices in Pakistani rupees (PKR).

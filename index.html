Questions and Answers

Online Quran Classes: Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers for parents and sisters before you book.

Can you really learn Quran online?
Yes. In a live 1-on-1 online class your teacher hears every letter you recite and corrects you on the spot, the same way she would in person. Students read from a shared screen, repeat after the teacher and practise between lessons. Most children and women read better online because the class is calm, private and fully focused on one student.

Are all your Quran teachers female?
Yes. Every teacher at Quran Online Tarteel is a woman. Each teacher is checked for recitation, Tajweed and teaching skill before she takes a class. Girls, boys and women all learn with a female teacher, so families can join with full peace of mind. Meet our female Quran teachers.

Who can join your online Quran classes?
Our classes are for children under 18 (boys and girls) and for adult women. We do not enrol adult men. This keeps every class taught by a female teacher and every learning space comfortable for mothers, daughters and young children.

How do the 3 free trial classes work?
You fill in the short form, we message you on WhatsApp within 24 hours and book three live classes at a time that suits you. There is no payment and no card needed. You meet your teacher, she checks the current level and you decide afterwards if you want to continue.

What age can my child start learning Quran online?
Most children are ready from around age 4 or 5, once they can sit for a short lesson and follow simple instructions. Young children start with Noorani Qaida in short, playful sessions. Older children are placed by level after the free trial, whether they need Qaida, Nazra reading or Tajweed.

Do I need to know Arabic to learn Quran online?
No. Complete beginners start from the Arabic letters with Noorani Qaida, and teachers explain everything in English. Step by step you learn to join letters, read words and then recite full verses. Many of our adult women students started without reading a single letter.

What do I need for online Quran classes?
You need a laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, a stable internet connection and a quiet corner. A headset helps the teacher hear your pronunciation clearly. We send the class link and any lesson pages before your first class.

How much do online Quran classes cost?
Fees depend on how many days a week you study and how long each class is. The first 3 classes are free. After the trial we share the monthly package that fits your schedule, and the full list of plans is on our online Quran classes fees page.

More questions? Read our FAQ and safeguarding policy or contact Quran Online Tarteel.

FAQs

Questions About Online Quran Classes

Are all classes online?

Yes. The academy offers online learning, not physical classes at an academy location.

Do you have female teachers?

Yes. The academy's classes are taught by female teachers.

Can children and adult women join?

Boys and girls under 18 may enquire. Learners aged 18 and above must be women.

Are free trial classes available?

Three free trial classes are offered. Enquire to discuss scheduling and availability.

What can I study?

Enquire about Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Tafseer, translation, Salah, duas, Hadith and Islamic studies.

How much do classes cost?

Contact the academy to discuss available packages and fees in PKR.

How do I book?

Submit the enquiry form or message the academy on WhatsApp. The academy must confirm availability and scheduling.

Yes. Classes are delivered online; there are no in-person classes.

Are female teachers available?

Yes. The academy provides female teachers.

Can children enrol?

Yes. Boys and girls under 18 can enrol with parent or guardian involvement.

Can adult men enrol?

No. For learners aged 18 and over, classes are available for women.

Are trial classes available?

Three free trial classes are available. Contact the academy to discuss arrangements.

What Nazra Means

What Is Nazra Quran Reading?

Nazra means reading the Quran by looking at the text, as opposed to reciting from memory. A Nazra course trains the student to read every word correctly, with the right vowels and rules, until whole pages flow without stopping.

Who should join?

Students who know the Arabic letters, usually after Noorani Qaida, but still read slowly or make mistakes.

Do I need Qaida first?

If you cannot yet recognise the letters and vowel signs, yes. Your teacher checks this in the free trial.

The Path

How Beginners Progress in Our Quran Reading Classes

A typical journey for students who join after Noorani Qaida.

  1. 1

    Short Surahs

    Start with the last surahs of the Quran, which most students already hear in salah.

  2. 2

    Juz Amma

    Read the 30th Juz fully, building speed and confidence.

  3. 3

    Longer Passages

    Move to Surah Al-Baqarah and beyond, reading a page or more each class.

  4. 4

    Fluent Reading

    Read any page of the Quran smoothly with correct vowels and stops.

Questions and Answers

Quran Reading for Beginners: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Quran Reading (Nazra) classes cost?

Quran Reading (Nazra) fees are monthly, per student: 30 minute classes are $15 for 2 days, $25 for 4 days, $30 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are $20 for 2 days, $30 for 4 days, $40 for 5 days a week. In Pakistan, 30 minute classes are Rs 4,000 for 2 days, Rs 6,500 for 4 days, Rs 8,000 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are Rs 5,000 for 2 days, Rs 8,000 for 4 days, Rs 10,000 for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

How long does it take to learn to read the Quran online?

With 4 to 5 classes a week, many students who have finished Qaida read fluently within 6 to 12 months. Adults with daily practice often progress faster.

Is this course for adults or kids?

Both. We teach children under 18 and adult women. Each student has their own one-to-one class at their own pace.

What is the difference between Nazra and Tajweed?

Nazra focuses on reading every word correctly and fluently. Tajweed goes deeper into pronunciation rules. Most students learn basic Tajweed during Nazra and then take a full Tajweed course.

Which Mushaf do you use?

We can teach from the Mushaf you use at home, whether it is the Indo-Pak script or the Madani (Uthmani) script.

Can I learn to read the Quran online for free?

Your first 2 classes are free. After that you choose a monthly plan. See our free trial page.

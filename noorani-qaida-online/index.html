What It Is

What Is Noorani Qaida?

Noorani Qaida is a beginner's book for learning to read Arabic the way it is written in the Quran. Instead of memorising words, students learn every letter, every vowel sign and every reading rule in order, so they can read any page of the Quran on their own.

Why start with Qaida instead of the Quran?

Skipping the basics leads to habits that are hard to fix later. A solid Qaida foundation makes reading and Tajweed much easier.

Who is it for?

Children from about age 4 and adults who cannot yet read Arabic. Many of our sisters start here too.

Questions and Answers

Noorani Qaida Online: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Noorani Qaida classes cost?

Noorani Qaida fees are monthly, per student: 30 minute classes cost $15 (Rs 3,500) for 2 days, $25 (Rs 6,000) for 4 days, $30 (Rs 7,500) for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes cost $20 (Rs 4,500) for 2 days, $30 (Rs 7,500) for 4 days, $35 (Rs 9,500) for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student can ask us about a family discount.

How long does it take to finish Noorani Qaida online?

It depends on age and practice. Many children finish in about 4 to 8 months with 4 to 5 short classes a week. Adults often move faster. Your teacher will give you an estimate after the free trial.

What age can a child start Noorani Qaida?

Most children can start around age 4 or 5. Lessons for very young children are kept gentle and playful, with little pauses, so they stay focused and enjoy learning.

Do I need to buy a Noorani Qaida book?

A printed copy helps for practice at home, but it is not required to start. Your teacher shares the pages on screen during class.

Can adults learn Noorani Qaida online?

Yes. Many women start with Noorani Qaida. Adults usually understand the rules quickly and finish sooner than children.

What comes after Noorani Qaida?

After Qaida, students start Quran reading (Nazra) and then Tajweed, and later Hifz for those who want to memorise.

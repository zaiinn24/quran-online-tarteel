About the Course

What Is Our Online Duas Course?

Duas are how we speak to Allah throughout the day: when we wake up, eat, leave the house, travel and go to sleep. Our online duas course teaches these short, beautiful duas one by one, with the Arabic, the meaning and the moment to say them.

Every dua we teach comes from the Quran or from authentic hadith, with its reference. Lessons are short and repetitive for children and calm and private for women.

Daily duas for kids

Children learn a new dua at a time through repetition, stories and practice, as part of Islamic studies for kids.

Duas for women

Sisters learn the morning and evening adhkar, duas from the Quran and duas for hard times, privately with a female teacher.

Questions and Answers

Daily Duas Course: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do online duas classes cost?

Daily Duas Course fees are monthly, per student: 30 minute classes are $15 for 2 days, $25 for 4 days, $30 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are $20 for 2 days, $30 for 4 days, $35 for 5 days a week. In Pakistan, 30 minute classes are Rs 3,500 for 2 days, Rs 6,000 for 4 days, Rs 7,500 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are Rs 4,500 for 2 days, Rs 7,500 for 4 days, Rs 9,500 for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

What are masnoon duas?

Masnoon duas are the duas taught by the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, for daily moments such as waking up, eating, leaving home and sleeping. We teach them with the Arabic, the meaning and their hadith reference.

Which duas should kids learn first?

Most children start with the duas they use every day: before and after eating, before sleeping and after waking up, and when entering and leaving the home. Your teacher adds new duas as your child grows.

Are the duas taught with meaning?

Yes. Every dua is taught with its meaning in English or Urdu, so children and women understand what they are asking Allah for.

Can duas be added to my child's Quran classes?

Yes. Many families add a few minutes of duas to each Quran class, or study duas on their own plan.

Do you teach the morning and evening adhkar?

Yes. Women and older children learn the morning and evening adhkar from authentic sources, with correct pronunciation and meaning.

About the Course

What Is Our Online Duas Course?

Duas are how we speak to Allah throughout the day: when we wake up, eat, leave the house, travel and go to sleep. Our online duas course teaches these short, beautiful duas one by one, with the Arabic, the meaning and the moment to say them.

Every dua we teach comes from the Quran or from authentic hadith, with its reference. Lessons are short and repetitive for children and calm and private for women.

Daily duas for kids

Children learn a new dua at a time through repetition, stories and practice, as part of Islamic studies for kids.

Duas for women

Sisters learn the morning and evening adhkar, duas from the Quran and duas for hard times, privately with a female teacher.

Questions and Answers

Daily Duas Course: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do online duas classes cost?

Fees depend on how many days a week you study and how long each class is. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share them with you. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

What are masnoon duas?

Masnoon duas are the duas taught by the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, for daily moments such as waking up, eating, leaving home and sleeping. We teach them with the Arabic, the meaning and their hadith reference. Browse our free duas and azkar library for the Arabic, transliteration and meaning of each one.

Which duas should kids learn first?

Most children start with the duas they use every day: before and after eating, before sleeping and after waking up, and when entering and leaving the home. Your teacher adds new duas as your child grows. See our daily duas for kids with meaning in English and Urdu, and read what dua is and how to make it.

Are the duas taught with meaning?

Yes. Every dua is taught with its meaning in English or Urdu, so children and women understand what they are asking Allah for. Understanding the meaning turns memorised words into a real conversation with Allah. Teachers explain each dua in English, Urdu or Arabic and link it to the right moment in the day.

Can duas be added to my child's Quran classes?

Yes. Many families add a few minutes of duas to each Quran class, or study duas on their own plan. Many families spend the last few minutes of each Quran class on one dua. See our online Quran classes for kids for how lessons are planned.

Do you teach the morning and evening adhkar?

Yes. Women and older children learn the morning and evening adhkar from authentic sources, with correct pronunciation and meaning. Read them on our morning and evening azkar page or download the free morning and evening adhkar PDF.

How many duas will my child learn?

Most children learn the everyday duas first, about 15 to 20, covering waking up, sleeping, eating, the toilet, leaving home and travel. Then they move on to the morning and evening adhkar and Rabbana duas from the Quran. The teacher adds one new dua at a time and revises the old ones every class.

Are the duas from authentic sources?

Yes. Every dua comes from the Quran or authentic hadith, and each one is taught with its reference, such as Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim or Hisn al-Muslim. You can check them in our free duas and azkar library, which lists the source of every dua.

Can women join the duas course?

Yes. Women learn the Arabic, pronunciation and meaning of daily duas, the adhkar and the Rabbana duas with a female teacher. Many sisters add the course to their Quran or Tajweed classes. See our Quran classes for women and what dhikr is.

How long are duas classes?

Classes are about 30 minutes, which is enough for one or two new duas plus revision. Children can join 2 or 4 days a week, or on weekends. Every student starts with 2 free trial classes, and after that fees are paid monthly in advance.

The Method

How Our Online Quran Memorization Classes Work

Hifz is not only about memorising new pages. The real challenge is keeping everything you have memorised. That is why every class balances three parts.

Not ready for full Hifz yet? Many children begin with Juz Amma memorization online, the short surahs of Amma Para, before moving on.

Sabaq: the new lesson

A few lines or a page, depending on the student, recited to the teacher until it is perfect.

Sabqi: recent revision

The lessons from the last few days, so new memorisation settles properly.

Manzil: older revision

A portion of everything memorised before, so nothing is forgotten.

Questions and Answers

Online Hifz Program: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do Hifz (Memorisation) classes cost?

Hifz (Memorisation) fees are monthly, per student: 25–30 minute classes are $30 for 2 days, $55 for 4 days, $75 for 5 days a week. In Pakistan, 25–30 minute classes are Rs 8,000 for 2 days, Rs 14,000 for 4 days, Rs 20,000 for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

How long does it take to memorise the whole Quran online?

It depends on daily practice and age. Full-time students often take 2 to 4 years, while part-time students may take longer. Consistency matters more than speed.

What is the best age to start Hifz?

Many children start Hifz between 7 and 12, once they read fluently. Adults can start at any age. The key is fluent reading and daily practice.

Can I do Hifz part time while at school or work?

Yes. Part-time plans with a smaller daily lesson and steady revision work well alongside school or work.

How do you make sure students do not forget?

Every class includes sabqi and manzil revision, not only new lessons. Teachers track what each student has memorised and plan revision cycles.

Is there a dua for memorising the Quran?

Many students ask Allah for ease and good memory in their own words. Your teacher can share commonly recited duas and good habits that help memorisation.

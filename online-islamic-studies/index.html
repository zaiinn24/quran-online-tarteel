Questions and Answers

Online Islamic Studies: Frequently Asked Questions

Can Islamic studies be combined with Quran classes?

Yes. Many families add 10 to 15 minutes of Islamic studies to each Quran class, or book one or two separate Islamic studies classes a week.

What age are your Islamic studies classes for?

We teach children from around age 5, teenagers and adult women. Topics and depth change with age.

Do you teach how to pray salah?

Yes. Students learn wudu, the positions of salah, what to recite in each position and how many rakats each prayer has, with practice in class.

Are classes taught in English?

Yes, lessons are explained in English. Arabic duas are taught with meaning and correct pronunciation.

Which school of thought do you follow?

Lessons focus on the agreed basics of worship. Where practice differs between schools, the teacher explains this kindly and follows what your family practises.

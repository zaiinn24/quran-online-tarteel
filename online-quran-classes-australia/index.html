How It Works

How Do Online Quran Classes Work for Families in Australia?

Online Quran classes in Australia are live video lessons between one student and one teacher. The student sees the Quran page on screen, listens to the teacher recite, repeats and is corrected straight away. Many Australian Muslim families learn online because a qualified female teacher nearby can be hard to find, especially outside the big cities. At Quran Online Tarteel, every teacher is female and every class is one-to-one.

Lessons that fit the Australian school day

Most Australian families book classes after school, in the evening or on weekends. Some prefer an early morning lesson before school. You choose the days and the time.

A clear path for every level

Children usually begin with Noorani Qaida online classes, then move to Quran reading, Tajweed and, when ready, Hifz. Adult women can start at any level.

Questions and Answers

Online Quran Classes in Australia: Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer online Quran classes in every Australian state?

Yes. Classes are online, so families in every state and territory can join, including regional and rural areas.

What time are the classes for Australian students?

You choose. Most families book after school, in the evening or on weekends, and some prefer a short lesson before school. Every class is scheduled in your own local time.

What about daylight saving time in Australia?

Some states change clocks in April and October and others do not. We reconfirm your class time with you on WhatsApp when clocks change, so the lesson stays at the local time you picked.

Are the teachers female?

Yes. Every teacher at Quran Online Tarteel is female. We teach children under 18, both boys and girls, and adult women. We do not enrol adult men.

Is there a free trial for families in Australia?

Yes. Your first 3 classes are free and no card is needed. After the trial you choose a monthly plan if you want to continue. See our fees and plans.

What do we need for the online class?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a stable internet connection, headphones if possible and a quiet place to sit. We send the class link before the first lesson.

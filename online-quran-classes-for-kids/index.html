How It Works for Children

What Are Online Quran Classes for Kids?

Online Quran classes for kids are live video lessons where one child learns with one teacher. Your child sees the Quran page on screen, hears the teacher recite, repeats after her and gets corrected on the spot. At Quran Online Tarteel, every teacher is female and every class is one-to-one.

Alongside Quran reading, children can memorize the short surahs with Juz Amma memorization, learn how to pray salah and the daily masnoon duas.

Short lessons that fit a child's attention

Young learners usually do best with 20 to 30 minute lessons. Older children can move to longer classes as their focus and level grow.

A path from first letter to fluent recitation

Children start with Noorani Qaida online classes, move on to Quran reading, then Tajweed and, for those who are ready, Hifz.

Questions and Answers

Quran Classes for Kids: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age to start online Quran classes for kids?

Most children are ready from around age 4 or 5, once they can sit for a short lesson and follow simple instructions. Younger children start with Noorani Qaida in short, playful sessions of 20 to 30 minutes.

Do boys also learn with female teachers?

Yes. All our teachers are female and they teach both boys and girls under 18. Many parents prefer a gentle female teacher for young children of any gender.

Can I watch my child's Quran class?

Yes. Parents are welcome to sit in on any lesson, and many do for the first few classes. Your child's teacher will also keep you updated on WhatsApp about how your child is doing.

How long is each class for kids?

Young children usually take 20 to 30 minute classes. Older children can take 30 to 45 minutes. The teacher suggests the right length after the free trial.

How many days a week should my child study?

Four or five days a week works well for most children, because short, regular lessons build reading faster than one long class. You choose the days that fit your family.

What does my child need for online Quran classes?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a stable internet connection, a headset if possible and a quiet corner. We send the class link before the first lesson.

Are your online Quran classes for kids free?

Your first 2 classes are completely free with no card needed. After that you choose a monthly plan. See our fees page for details.

Questions and Answers

Quran Classes for Women: Frequently Asked Questions

Are your online Quran classes only for women?

Our adult classes are for women only. We also teach children under 18, both boys and girls. Adult men are not enrolled, and every teacher is female.

I am an adult and cannot read Arabic. Can I still learn?

Yes. Many of our students start as complete beginners. You begin with Noorani Qaida, learning the letters and sounds step by step, and with regular practice you move on to reading the Quran itself, at a pace that suits you.

Can I keep my camera off during class?

Yes. For sisters, the camera is completely your choice. Your teacher keeps her own camera off during lessons and listens closely to every letter you recite. If you would like to be sure your teacher is a woman, she will happily turn her camera on once so you can see for yourself.

What times are classes available?

We schedule classes around your time zone, including early mornings, evenings and weekends. Tell us your preferred times in the form and we will match you with a teacher.

Can I learn Hifz as a working woman?

Yes. Part-time Hifz plans with a few lines a day and steady revision work well around work and family. Your teacher sets a pace you can keep.

Can my daughter and I study together?

Yes. Many mothers and daughters join together, each with her own one-to-one class or back to back slots with the same teacher. Just select both in the form.

How It Works

How Do Online Quran Classes Work in Pakistan?

Online Quran classes in Pakistan are live video lessons in which one student learns to read, recite or memorise the Quran with one teacher over the internet. Quran Online Tarteel is an online Quran academy where every teacher is female and every class is one-to-one, so your daughter, son or you learn at home with full attention on every letter.

A female Quran teacher without a home visit

With online classes, nobody needs to come to your house and nobody needs to travel. The teacher appears on screen at the booked time, and the lesson ends when the class ends.

Who can join from Pakistan

Boys and girls under 18 and adult women can join, from first letters to fluent recitation. We do not enrol adult men, which keeps every class comfortable for families and ladies.

Questions and Answers

Online Quran Classes in Pakistan: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do online Quran classes cost in Pakistan?

Monthly fees in Pakistan start at Rs 3,500 for Noorani Qaida, 2 days a week with 30 minute classes. Quran reading (Nazra) is Rs 4,000 for 2 days, Rs 6,500 for 4 days and Rs 8,000 for 5 days a week. Every new student starts with 2 free classes, so you pay nothing until you decide to continue. See the full fees table for every course.

Can I get a female Quran teacher at home in Pakistan?

Yes, online. Every teacher at Quran Online Tarteel is female and teaches live one-to-one over video, so you learn at home without anyone visiting your house. Ladies and children see the teacher on screen, read the Quran page together and are corrected on every letter. Read more about our female Quran teacher service.

Are there online Quran classes for ladies in Pakistan?

Yes. Ladies of any age in Pakistan can learn to read the Quran, improve Tajweed, memorise surahs or study translation and Tafseer with a female teacher. Classes are private and one-to-one, and many ladies book morning classes when the house is quiet. We do not enrol adult men. See our online Quran classes for women.

Do you offer an online Tajweed course in Pakistan?

Yes. Our online Tajweed course covers makharij, the qualities of letters and the main rules such as noon sakinah, meem sakinah and madd, taught one-to-one with practice on real verses. It suits children who already read and ladies who want to correct their recitation. See our online Tajweed classes and the free Tajweed rules for beginners guide.

What class timings are available in Pakistan?

Classes run in the morning, afternoon, evening and at weekends, all in Pakistan time. Ladies often choose mornings, children usually study after school or after tuition, and older students prefer evenings. Pakistan does not change its clocks, so your class time stays the same all year. Tell us your preferred time on the form.

How do I pay the fee in Pakistan?

You can pay by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer. The fee is paid monthly, in advance, and we share the details on WhatsApp after your 2 free classes. See fees and plans for every course in rupees.

What happens if there is load-shedding or an internet problem?

If load-shedding or an internet problem stops a class at the teacher's end, we always make the class up, at the weekend or on any weekday your child is free. If the problem is at your end, please message your teacher as early as you can. Those classes are not automatically made up, but she will try her best to fit one in.

Are classes taught in Urdu?

Yes. Our teachers explain in Urdu or English, whichever your child is more comfortable with. The Quran is always read in Arabic.

Are classes held on public holidays and in Ramadan?

Classes are off on Eid and on Pakistan's government holidays, such as 9th and 10th Muharram and 12 Rabi ul Awwal, and these days are not made up. In Ramadan the class timings change, and we agree new times with you.

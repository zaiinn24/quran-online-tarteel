How It Works

What Are Online Quran Classes in the UK?

Online Quran classes in the UK are live video lessons in which one student learns to read, recite or memorise the Quran with one teacher over the internet. Instead of travelling to an evening madrasah, your child logs in from home, sees the Quran page on screen and is corrected on every letter. At Quran Online Tarteel, every teacher is female and every lesson is one-to-one.

What an online Quran tutor does in each lesson

An online Quran tutor listens, corrects and plans. Each 30 or 45 minute lesson starts with revision, moves on to something new and ends with practice for next time, so progress builds week by week.

Who can enrol from the UK

Boys and girls under 18 and adult women can enrol, from complete beginners to fluent readers. We do not teach adult men, which keeps every lesson comfortable for children, mums and older women.

Questions and Answers

Online Quran Classes in UK: Frequently Asked Questions

How much are online Quran classes in the UK?

Fees depend on how many days a week the student studies and whether lessons are 30 or 45 minutes. Plans are monthly, with 2, 4 or 5 days a week. Message us on WhatsApp or fill in the form and we will send the current monthly fee for your plan. Every new student starts with 2 free classes, so you pay nothing until you decide to continue. See fees and plans.

What are the best online Quran classes in the UK?

The best online Quran classes in the UK are one-to-one, taught by a teacher who corrects every letter, booked at UK times and backed by WhatsApp updates for parents. A free trial lets you check all of this before paying. Quran Online Tarteel meets each point, and every teacher is female, for boys and girls alike. Read about our online Quran classes for kids.

Do you offer online Quran classes for adults in the UK?

Yes, for adult women only. Women of any age in the UK can learn to read the Quran, improve their Tajweed, memorise surahs or study translation and Tafseer with a female teacher. We do not teach adult men. Lessons are private and one-to-one, so complete beginners can start from the alphabet without feeling rushed. See our online Quran classes for women.

Can I find a female online Quran tutor in the UK?

Yes. Every online Quran tutor at Quran Online Tarteel is female, and each one teaches one student at a time. You can request a tutor when you book your free trial, and we match her to your preferred UK times. Find out how we choose our teachers on the female Quran teacher page.

What times are online Quran classes available in the UK?

Lessons are available after school, in the early evening, during weekday daytime and at weekends, all in UK time. When the clocks change between GMT and BST in March and October, we reconfirm your lesson time on WhatsApp so nothing is missed. Tell us your preferred times on the form and we will match a teacher.

Is online Quran tuition better than going to madrasah?

Online Quran tuition is not better in every way, but it does give one-to-one attention, no travel and a pace set for your child. A local madrasah gives community and friendships. Many UK families choose online lessons for reading and Tajweed, then keep the mosque for Jumuah, events and time with other Muslim children.

Can my son learn with a female Quran teacher?

Yes. Boys under 18 learn with our female teachers in the same way girls do. Many parents prefer a female teacher for young boys because lessons feel gentle and patient. Parents can sit in on any lesson, and the class link is shared with the parent, never with the child alone. Read our FAQ and safeguarding page.

What does my child need for online Quran lessons?

Your child needs a laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, a steady internet connection and a quiet place to sit. Headphones help with hearing pronunciation clearly. The teacher shares the Qaida or Quran page on screen, so you do not need to buy any books before the free trial begins.

How do I book free online Quran classes in the UK?

Fill in the short form on this page or message us on WhatsApp with the student's age, level and your UK town or city.

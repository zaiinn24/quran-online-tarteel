How It Works

What Are Online Quran Classes in the USA?

Online Quran classes in the USA are live video lessons in which one student learns to read, recite or memorize the Quran with one teacher over the internet. The student sees the Mushaf page on screen, listens to the teacher, repeats and is corrected straight away. At Quran Online Tarteel, every class is one-to-one, every teacher is female, and only children and women are enrolled.

How a live one-to-one lesson works

Each lesson is 30 or 45 minutes. The teacher starts by revising the last lesson, teaches a new part, listens to the student read it back and sets short practice for next time. Parents receive the class link and can sit beside their child.

Who can join from the USA

Boys and girls under 18 and adult women of any age can join, from complete beginners to fluent readers. We do not enroll adult men, which keeps every class comfortable for families and women.

Questions and Answers

Online Quran Classes in USA: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do online Quran classes cost in the USA?

Monthly fees in the USA start at $15 for Noorani Qaida, 2 days a week with 30 minute classes. Quran reading (Nazra) is $15 for 2 days, $25 for 4 days and $30 for 5 days a week. It is the same fair fee we charge in Pakistan, simply converted to your currency. Every new student starts with 2 free classes, so you pay nothing until you decide to continue. See the full fees table for every course.

What are the best online Quran classes for kids in the USA?

The best online Quran classes for kids in the USA are one-to-one, taught by a patient teacher who corrects every mistake, and scheduled around the school day. Look for a clear path from Noorani Qaida to Tajweed, WhatsApp updates for parents and a free trial. Quran Online Tarteel offers all of these, with only female teachers for boys and girls. Read more about our online Quran classes for kids.

Are there online Quran classes for adults in the USA?

Yes, for adult women only. Women of any age in the USA can learn to read the Quran, improve Tajweed, memorize surahs or study translation and Tafseer with a female teacher. We do not enroll adult men. Classes are private and one-to-one, so a complete beginner can start from the Arabic letters without feeling rushed. See our online Quran classes for women.

Which US time zones do you teach in?

We teach families in every US time zone: Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska and Hawaii. When you book, tell us your city and preferred time, and we match a teacher to your local schedule. When clocks change in March and November, we reconfirm your class time on WhatsApp so it stays at the local time you chose.

How many days a week should my child study in the USA?

Most children do well with 4 or 5 short lessons a week, because regular practice helps them remember letters and rules. Busy families can start with 2 days, and children doing Noorani Qaida or Hifz often move faster with 5 days. The teacher suggests a plan after the free trial, and you can change it from the next month.

How do I pay for online Quran classes from the USA?

Families abroad usually pay by bank transfer or Western Union, or a relative in Pakistan pays for them by JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer. The fee is paid monthly, in advance, and we share the payment details on WhatsApp after your 2 free classes. We do not take card or PayPal payments at the moment. See fees and plans for the dollar fees.

Can classes fit around school and weekends in the USA?

Yes. Most American families choose weekday evenings after school or weekend mornings, in Eastern, Central, Mountain or Pacific time. Tell us the times that suit your family in the form and we will match you with a teacher who is free then.

What happens to my class time when US clocks change?

Daylight saving moves the time difference with Pakistan by one hour. Message us on WhatsApp before the clocks change and we will confirm your class time with your teacher. If the same local time is not free, we offer the nearest time she is available.

Can my child learn in English?

Yes. Our teachers teach in English and Urdu, so children who grew up in America can follow every explanation easily. The Quran itself is always read in Arabic.

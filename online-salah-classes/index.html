What Are Online Salah Classes?

Salah is the second pillar of Islam and the first thing we will be asked about. Our online salah classes teach children and women the complete prayer in a calm, one-to-one setting, so nothing is skipped and no question feels too small.

Your teacher demonstrates each step, listens to your recitation and corrects you kindly. Lessons focus on the agreed basics of prayer. Where practice differs between schools, the teacher follows what your family practises.

What You Learn

What You Learn in Our Namaz Classes

Every lesson is live, one-to-one and matched to the student's age and level.

و

Wudu (Ablution)

Each step of wudu in order, and what breaks it, practised in class.

ص

The Positions of Salah

Standing, ruku, sujood and sitting, with the correct movements for each.

ف

What to Recite

Takbir, Surah Al-Fatiha, a short surah, the words of ruku and sujood, tashahhud and salam, with correct pronunciation.

ر

Rakats of Each Prayer

Fajr 2, Dhuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3 and Isha 4 fard rakats. See our how many rakats guide.

م

Meaning of the Words

Understand what you are saying in salah, explained in English or Urdu.

ق

Short Surahs for Salah

Memorize short surahs to recite in prayer with our Juz Amma memorization classes.

Questions and Answers

Online Salah Classes: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do online salah classes cost?

Salah (Prayer) Lessons fees are monthly, per student: 30 minute classes are $15 for 2 days, $25 for 4 days, $30 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are $20 for 2 days, $30 for 4 days, $35 for 5 days a week. In Pakistan, 30 minute classes are Rs 3,500 for 2 days, Rs 6,000 for 4 days, Rs 7,500 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are Rs 4,500 for 2 days, Rs 7,500 for 4 days, Rs 9,500 for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

Can I really learn how to pray salah online?

Yes. In a live one-to-one class your teacher watches each step, listens to your recitation and corrects you on the spot. Young children keep their camera on so the teacher can guide their movements, and for women the camera is optional.

What age should a child start learning salah?

Many families begin gently around age 7, and younger children often enjoy copying their parents before that. Our teachers adapt each lesson to your child's age.

Do I need to know Arabic to learn salah?

No. Your teacher teaches the Arabic words of the prayer slowly, with their meaning in English or Urdu, until you can recite them correctly.

Which school of thought do you follow for salah?

Lessons focus on the agreed basics of prayer. Where practice differs between schools, your teacher explains kindly and follows what your family practises.

Are salah classes available for new Muslim sisters?

Yes. New Muslim sisters learn salah privately with a female teacher, starting from wudu and the shahada, at a gentle pace. See our classes for new Muslims.

The Basics

What Is Tajweed and Why Does It Matter?

Tajweed comes from an Arabic root meaning to make something better. In recitation it means giving every letter its right: the correct point of articulation (makhraj), its qualities (sifaat) and the rules that apply when letters meet.

Meaning can change with pronunciation

Some Arabic letters sound close to each other. Mixing them up can change the meaning of a word, which is why Tajweed is taught with such care.

Beauty and confidence in salah

Students who learn Tajweed recite with more confidence in prayer and enjoy listening to and reciting the Quran more.

Questions and Answers

Online Tajweed Classes: Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to learn Tajweed online?

Most students learn the core rules in about 3 to 6 months with regular classes. Applying them smoothly while reading takes ongoing practice, which continues as you read more of the Quran.

Can I learn Tajweed online without knowing Arabic?

You need to read Arabic letters first. If you cannot, start with Noorani Qaida. You do not need to understand the Arabic language to learn Tajweed.

Are your Tajweed classes for kids too?

Yes. Children learn Tajweed in simple words with lots of repetition. Adults and teenagers can go into more detail on each rule.

Is a colour-coded Tajweed Quran helpful?

Yes, many beginners find a colour-coded Tajweed Mushaf helpful because it highlights where rules apply. It is optional, and your teacher will explain how to use it.

Do you teach makharij in detail?

Yes. Correct makharij are the foundation of Tajweed. Your teacher checks each letter's point of articulation before moving on to rules.

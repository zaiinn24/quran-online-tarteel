Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Quran Online Tarteel (quranonlinetarteel.net) provides live online Quran classes for children and women. You can contact us at info@quranonlinetarteel.net or on WhatsApp at +92 334 5259952.

What we collect

When you fill in a form we collect the details you give us, such as your name, WhatsApp number, email, country, who will learn, the course you are interested in and your message.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and arrange free trial classes
  • To schedule and run your classes
  • To keep parents updated about their child's classes

We do not sell your information.

How forms are delivered

Form submissions are sent to our email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form delivery service. They are used only to contact you about classes.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, for example which pages are visited. Google Analytics uses cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Classes for children are arranged with a parent or guardian. Please ask a parent to fill in the form for a child.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your details at any time by email or WhatsApp.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.

About the Course

Online Islam Classes for New Muslim Sisters

Becoming Muslim is a beautiful beginning, and it can also feel like there is a lot to learn. Our new Muslim classes take away the pressure. You learn privately, one-to-one, with a female teacher who answers every question kindly and never makes you feel rushed.

We start with what you need first: the meaning of the shahada, how to make wudu and how to pray. Then, at your own pace, you learn to read the Quran from the Arabic letters with Noorani Qaida.

For your children too

Children of revert families learn alongside you, with their own friendly lessons, so the whole family grows in faith together. These classes are for sisters and children only.

Questions and Answers

Classes for New Muslims: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do new Muslim classes cost?

Fees depend on how many days a week you study and how long each class is. Message us on WhatsApp and we will share them with you. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

Are your classes for new Muslims only for women?

Yes. Our classes are for revert sisters and for children under 18. Every teacher is a woman. We do not teach adult men. Revert sisters and their children, both girls and boys under 18, are welcome. Meet our female Quran teachers.

I don't know any Arabic. Can I still learn?

Yes. Many new Muslims start with no Arabic at all. Your teacher teaches in English, starting with what you need for salah, and you learn to read the Arabic letters step by step. Our teachers explain everything in English, Urdu or Arabic, and the very first lessons are only letters and sounds. Many reverts are surprised how quickly the letters become familiar with a patient teacher and short daily practice.

What should a new Muslim learn first?

Most sisters start with the meaning of the shahada, wudu and how to pray, including Surah Al-Fatiha. After that comes Quran reading and everyday Islam, at your own pace. Read how to pray salah step by step and how to perform wudu for the basics you can start this week.

Can my children join the classes too?

Yes. Children of revert families can learn with their own teacher or alongside you, with lessons suited to their age. Children have their own one to one classes with a female teacher, and many families book them back to back. See our online Quran classes for kids.

Are classes private?

Yes. Every class is one-to-one with a female teacher. For women the camera is optional, classes are never recorded and your details are never shared. Classes are one to one, never recorded, and you control your camera. Read our FAQ and safeguarding page for the full privacy rules.

How long does it take a new Muslim to learn salah?

Many new Muslims can pray on their own within a few weeks, starting with the movements and Al-Fatiha. Learning all the words with meaning usually takes 2 to 3 months of regular classes. Read how to pray salah step by step for a simple first-month plan.

Will I learn to read the Quran in Arabic?

Yes. You start with the Arabic letters through Noorani Qaida, then move to reading the Quran itself. Many reverts read slowly within 6 to 9 months of regular classes. Meanwhile, your teacher helps you memorise Al-Fatiha and short surahs for salah.

Can I learn the meaning of what I recite?

Yes. Teachers explain Al-Fatiha and the short surahs word by word in English, Urdu or Arabic, so your salah becomes meaningful from the start. Later you can join our translation and Tafseer classes and read our guide to the best English translation of the Quran.

How do I start classes as a new Muslim?

Fill in the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you already know, even if it is nothing, and when you are free. We reply within 24 hours and arrange 2 free trial classes with a patient female teacher. After the trial, fees are paid monthly in advance.

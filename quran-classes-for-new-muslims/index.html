Online Islam Classes for New Muslim Sisters

Becoming Muslim is a beautiful beginning, and it can also feel like there is a lot to learn. Our new Muslim classes take away the pressure. You learn privately, one-to-one, with a female teacher who answers every question kindly and never makes you feel rushed.

Questions and Answers

Classes for New Muslims: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do new Muslim classes cost?

Classes for New Muslims fees are monthly, per student: 30 minute classes are $15 for 2 days, $25 for 4 days, $30 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are $20 for 2 days, $30 for 4 days, $35 for 5 days a week. In Pakistan, 30 minute classes are Rs 3,500 for 2 days, Rs 6,000 for 4 days, Rs 7,500 for 5 days a week; 45 minute classes are Rs 4,500 for 2 days, Rs 7,500 for 4 days, Rs 9,500 for 5 days a week. The first 2 classes are free, and families with more than one student get a family discount.

Are your classes for new Muslims only for women?

Yes. Our classes are for revert sisters and for children under 18. Every teacher is a woman. We do not teach adult men.

I don't know any Arabic. Can I still learn?

Yes. Many new Muslims start with no Arabic at all. Your teacher teaches in English, starting with what you need for salah, and you learn to read the Arabic letters step by step.

What should a new Muslim learn first?

Most sisters start with the meaning of the shahada, wudu and how to pray, including Surah Al-Fatiha. After that comes Quran reading and everyday Islam, at your own pace.

Can my children join the classes too?

Yes. Children of revert families can learn with their own teacher or alongside you, with lessons suited to their age.

Are classes private?

Yes. Every class is one-to-one with a female teacher. For women the camera is optional, classes are never recorded and your details are never shared.

How Many Rakats in Each Prayer?

A simple chart of the fard rakats in the five daily prayers, for children and new learners.

Every Muslim prays five obligatory (fard) prayers each day. This chart shows how many fard rakats each prayer has, which all schools of thought agree on. It is a handy reference for children and new learners.

Want a teacher to guide you through every step? Join our online salah classes with a female teacher.

Fard Rakats in the Five Daily Prayers

PrayerTimeFard rakats
FajrDawn, before sunrise2
DhuhrAfter midday4
AsrAfternoon4
MaghribJust after sunset3
IshaNight4

That makes 17 fard rakats in a day.

What About Sunnah and Witr?

Alongside the fard prayers, the Prophet, peace be upon him, regularly prayed extra sunnah rakats, and Witr is prayed after Isha. The number of sunnah rakats and how Witr is prayed differ slightly between schools of thought, so follow what your family and local scholars practise. Your teacher can explain this in class.

Teaching Children to Pray

  • Start by praying next to your child so they copy the movements
  • Teach the short surahs and words of salah a little at a time
  • Use this chart on the wall as a daily reminder

How Many Rakats: Questions Answered

How many rakats are in each prayer?

Fard rakats: Fajr 2, Dhuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3 and Isha 4, a total of 17 each day.

How many rakats is Maghrib?

Maghrib has 3 fard rakats.

How many rakats is Isha?

Isha has 4 fard rakats, followed by sunnah and Witr according to your school of thought.

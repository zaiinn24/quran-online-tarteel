Dua is calling upon Allah: asking Him for what you need, thanking Him and turning to Him in hope and fear. The word دعاء comes from an Arabic root meaning to call or invite. The Prophet, peace be upon him, called dua worship itself. This guide explains the meaning of dua in Islam, its etiquette, the best times to ask and how to make dua step by step.

:::key
- **Dua meaning:** a personal call to Allah, in any language, at any time.
- The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: **"Dua is worship"** (Abu Dawud 1479, At-Tirmidhi 2969).
- Begin with **praise of Allah and salawat on the Prophet**, ask with certainty and do not rush the answer.
- Special times include **the last third of the night, between adhan and iqamah, in sujood and on Fridays**.
:::

## What Does Dua Mean in Islam?

In Islam, dua is any sincere call to Allah. It can be a long, tearful request in the night or a single word whispered on the way to work.

### Dua is worship

Allah says:

:::quote
ayah: 40:60
en: And your Lord says: Call upon Me; I will respond to you.
:::

Because dua shows our need for Allah and His power over everything, it is one of the purest forms of worship.

### Dua and dhikr

Dhikr is remembering and praising Allah, while dua is asking Him. They go together: most duas of the Prophet, peace be upon him, begin with praise. Read more in our guide to [what dhikr is](/blog/what-is-dhikr/).

### Types of dua

Scholars describe two types: dua of worship (praising Allah, like *SubhanAllah*) and dua of request (asking for something). Both are rewarded.

## The Etiquette of Making Dua

There are no strict conditions for dua, but the Quran and Sunnah teach manners that make it more likely to be accepted.

| Etiquette | What to do | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sincerity | Ask Allah alone, with a present heart | Quran 40:14 |
| Praise first | Begin with praise of Allah and salawat on the Prophet | At-Tirmidhi 3477 |
| Certainty | Ask believing Allah will answer | At-Tirmidhi 3479 |
| Patience | Do not say "I asked and was not answered" | Sahih al-Bukhari 6340 |
| Halal life | Lawful food and earnings | Sahih Muslim 1015 |
| Raise the hands | Palms up, facing the sky | Abu Dawud 1488 |

### Ask with certainty

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said to call upon Allah while being certain of the answer, because Allah does not answer the dua of a heart that is heedless (At-Tirmidhi 3479).

### Never give up

Every sincere dua is answered in one of three ways: Allah gives what you asked, keeps it as reward for the Hereafter, or turns away an equal harm (Musnad Ahmad 11133). So keep asking.

## Best Times for Dua to Be Accepted

Allah hears every dua, at every time, but the Sunnah names moments of special acceptance.

:::cards
- **The last third of the night** | Allah descends to the lowest heaven and says: who is calling Me that I may answer him (Sahih al-Bukhari 1145).
- **In sujood** | The servant is closest to his Lord while prostrating, so make plenty of dua (Sahih Muslim 482).
- **Between adhan and iqamah** | The dua made in this time is not rejected (Abu Dawud 521, At-Tirmidhi 212).
- **On Friday** | There is an hour on Friday in which a Muslim asks for good and it is given (Sahih al-Bukhari 935).
- **While fasting and at iftar** | The fasting person is among those whose dua is not rejected (At-Tirmidhi 3598).
- **On Laylatul Qadr** | See our guide to the [last ten nights of Ramadan](/blog/laylatul-qadr-last-ten-nights/).
:::

### Times of hardship

Allah answers the one in distress who calls on Him (Quran 27:62). Our [duas for anxiety and stress](/duas-azkar/duas-for-anxiety/) collects the Prophet's duas for hard moments.

### While travelling and for parents

The dua of the traveller and the dua of a parent for a child are mentioned among those answered (At-Tirmidhi 1905). See the [travel dua](/duas-azkar/travel-dua/).

## How to Make Dua Step by Step

There is no single required format, but this simple order follows the Sunnah.

:::steps
1. **Be in a state of purity if you can**, face the qiblah and raise your hands.
2. **Praise Allah**, for example with *Alhamdulillah* or one of His names that fits your need.
3. **Send salawat on the Prophet**, peace be upon him, such as *Allahumma salli ala Muhammad*.
4. **Admit your need and your mistakes.** Seek forgiveness with *Astaghfirullah*.
5. **Ask for what you need**, in your own words and language, for this life and the next. Include your parents and the believers.
6. **Close with praise and salawat**, and trust that Allah heard you.
:::

### Can I make dua in English or Urdu?

Yes. Personal dua can be in any language. Inside the fard prayer, many scholars prefer duas in Arabic, so learning a few Quranic duas helps.

## Teaching Children to Make Dua

Children learn dua best when they see parents asking Allah for everyday things.

### Make it part of the day

Teach a dua for each routine: waking, eating, leaving home and sleeping. Our page of [daily duas for kids](/duas-azkar/daily-duas-for-kids/) has each one with meaning.

### Let them ask in their own words

Encourage children to tell Allah about their day and ask for help with a test or a friend. This builds a personal relationship with Allah, not just memorised words. Our [online duas course](/online-duas-course/) and [Islamic studies online](/online-islamic-studies/) classes teach both.

:::faq
Q: What is the meaning of dua in Islam?
A: Dua means calling upon Allah, asking Him for help, forgiveness or anything good, and praising Him. It comes from an Arabic root meaning to call. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said that dua is worship, as narrated by Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi. Dua can be made in any language, at any time, about any lawful need, big or small.
Q: What is the difference between dua and salah?
A: Salah is the formal prayer with fixed times, movements and words, obligatory five times a day. Dua is a personal request to Allah that you can make at any time, in any words and any language. Salah contains duas, such as Al-Fatiha and the dua before salam, and the moment of sujood is one of the best times to make dua.
Q: When is dua most likely to be accepted?
A: Authentic hadith mention the last third of the night, sujood in prayer, the time between adhan and iqamah, a special hour on Friday, while fasting, while travelling and on Laylatul Qadr. Duas of the oppressed and of parents for their children are also mentioned. Ask with sincerity and certainty at these times, but remember Allah hears every dua at every moment.
Q: Can I make dua for anything I want?
A: You can ask Allah for anything that is good and lawful, for this world and the Hereafter, however small it seems. You should not ask for something sinful or for cutting family ties, as the Prophet, peace be upon him, warned. It is also from the Sunnah to include your parents, family and all believers in your dua.
Q: Why is my dua not answered?
A: The Prophet, peace be upon him, taught that every sincere dua is answered in one of three ways: Allah gives what you asked, saves the reward for the Hereafter, or turns away a similar harm, as narrated by Ahmad. Keep asking with patience, avoid haram income, and do not say you prayed and were not answered.
:::

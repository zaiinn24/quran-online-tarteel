Sunnah prayers are the voluntary rakats the Prophet, peace be upon him, prayed before or after the five obligatory prayers. They are not fard, but they bring great reward and make up for shortcomings in the fard. This guide explains the 12 rakats of sunnah muakkadah, gives a full chart for every prayer, explains Witr and covers other voluntary prayers such as Duha and Tahajjud.

:::key
- **Fard** rakats are obligatory. **Sunnah** rakats are voluntary prayers the Prophet, peace be upon him, prayed.
- **Sunnah muakkadah** (emphasised) are the ones he almost never left, especially **12 rakats a day**.
- Whoever prays 12 sunnah rakats daily, **a house is built for them in Paradise** (Sahih Muslim 728).
- Counts differ slightly between schools of thought. **Follow your family's madhab and your teacher.**
:::

## What Are Sunnah Prayers?

The word sunnah means the way or practice of the Prophet, peace be upon him. In salah, sunnah prayers are the rakats he prayed in addition to the fard.

### Sunnah muakkadah and ghair muakkadah

Sunnah muakkadah are emphasised sunnah prayers the Prophet, peace be upon him, prayed regularly. Ghair muakkadah (non-emphasised) are those he prayed sometimes. Missing a sunnah prayer is not a sin, but leaving the emphasised ones habitually means missing great reward.

### Why sunnah prayers matter

On the Day of Judgement, if the fard prayers are lacking, Allah will say: see if My servant has any voluntary prayers to complete them (Abu Dawud 864, At-Tirmidhi 413). Sunnah prayers protect your fard.

## The 12 Rakats of Sunnah Muakkadah

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Whoever prays twelve rakats in a day and night, a house will be built for him in Paradise" (Sahih Muslim 728). At-Tirmidhi (415) explained them:

:::cards 3
- **2 before Fajr** | The most emphasised. Better than the world and all it contains (Sahih Muslim 725).
- **4 before Dhuhr** | Prayed as two sets of two or one set of four, depending on the school.
- **2 after Dhuhr** | Prayed after the fard of Dhuhr.
- **2 after Maghrib** | Prayed after the 3 fard rakats.
- **2 after Isha** | Prayed after the 4 fard rakats, before Witr.
- **Total: 12** | 2 + 4 + 2 + 2 + 2 every day and night.
:::

### A version with 10 rakats

Ibn Umar reported 10 rakats, with 2 rather than 4 before Dhuhr (Sahih al-Bukhari 1180). Many scholars say both are sunnah, so praying 4 before Dhuhr earns the fuller reward.

### Praying sunnah at home

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said the best prayer of a person is in his house, except the fard prayers (Sahih al-Bukhari 731). Many families pray the sunnah at home, which also teaches children.

## Full Chart: Fard, Sunnah and Witr for Each Prayer

The chart below shows a common practice, as widely followed in South Asia and in the Hanafi school, with notes where other schools differ.

| Prayer | Sunnah before | Fard | Sunnah after | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fajr | 2 (muakkadah) | 2 | None | No voluntary prayer after Fajr until sunrise |
| Dhuhr | 4 (muakkadah) | 4 | 2 (muakkadah) | Some schools pray 2 before |
| Asr | 4 (ghair muakkadah) | 4 | None | No voluntary prayer after Asr until sunset |
| Maghrib | None | 3 | 2 (muakkadah) | Some pray 2 short rakats before, which is also reported |
| Isha | 4 (ghair muakkadah) | 4 | 2 (muakkadah) | Followed by Witr |
| Witr | | 3 (Hanafi) | | Other schools: 1, 3, 5 or more, odd number |

### Asr sunnah

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "May Allah have mercy on a person who prays four before Asr" (Abu Dawud 1271, At-Tirmidhi 430). It is recommended, not emphasised.

### The full count for Isha

People often ask how many rakats Isha has in total. In the Hanafi practice above: 4 sunnah, 4 fard, 2 sunnah, 3 Witr, and many add 2 voluntary rakats. The fard is always 4. For the fard-only chart, see [how many rakats in each prayer](/resources/how-many-rakats/).

## Witr Prayer Explained

Witr means odd. It is the odd-numbered prayer that closes the night prayers.

### Is Witr obligatory?

In the Hanafi school, Witr is wajib (necessary). In the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools it is a very emphasised sunnah. All agree you should not leave it. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Make Witr the last of your prayer at night" (Sahih al-Bukhari 998).

### How Witr is prayed

Witr can be prayed after Isha until Fajr. In the Hanafi way it is 3 rakats with one salam and Dua al-Qunoot in the third rakat before ruku. Others pray it as 2 plus 1, or as a single rakat. Learn the method your teacher follows.

## Other Voluntary Prayers

Beyond the daily sunnah, there are voluntary (nafl) prayers with special virtues.

### Duha (Chasht)

Prayed after sunrise until just before Dhuhr, at least 2 rakats. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said two rakats of Duha are enough as charity for every joint of the body (Sahih Muslim 720).

### Tahajjud (night prayer)

Prayed after Isha and some sleep, before Fajr, in sets of two. The best prayer after the fard is the night prayer (Sahih Muslim 1163). The last third of the night is also a special time for [dua](/blog/what-is-dua/).

### Tahiyyat al-Masjid and after wudu

When you enter a mosque, pray 2 rakats before sitting (Sahih al-Bukhari 444). Two rakats after wudu are also recommended. See [how to perform wudu](/blog/how-to-perform-wudu/).

## Helping Children Learn the Sunnah Prayers

Children first need the fard prayers to become a habit. Sunnah can be added one prayer at a time.

### Start with Fajr and Maghrib sunnah

These are short and easy to remember. Praise children for praying them, and pray together where possible.

### Learn the words with meaning

When children understand what they are saying, prayer stops feeling mechanical. Our [online salah classes](/online-salah-classes/) and [Islamic studies online](/online-islamic-studies/) teach the meaning of each part. After salah, add the [azkar after namaz](/duas-azkar/azkar-after-namaz/).

:::faq
Q: What are the sunnah prayers in Islam?
A: Sunnah prayers are voluntary rakats the Prophet, peace be upon him, prayed before or after the five fard prayers. The most emphasised are 12 daily rakats: 2 before Fajr, 4 before Dhuhr, 2 after Dhuhr, 2 after Maghrib and 2 after Isha. Sahih Muslim reports that whoever prays these 12 rakats daily will have a house built for them in Paradise.
Q: How many sunnah rakats are in Isha?
A: Isha has 2 emphasised sunnah rakats after the 4 fard rakats. Many Muslims, especially in the Hanafi school, also pray 4 non-emphasised sunnah rakats before the fard, then Witr after the 2 sunnah. So a common complete Isha is 4 sunnah, 4 fard, 2 sunnah and 3 Witr. The fard part is always 4 rakats.
Q: Is it a sin to skip sunnah prayers?
A: No. Missing a sunnah prayer is not a sin, because sunnah prayers are voluntary. However, the emphasised sunnah, especially the 2 rakats before Fajr, were prayed almost constantly by the Prophet, peace be upon him, so leaving them regularly means missing great reward. Sunnah prayers also help make up for any shortcomings in the fard prayers.
Q: Can I pray sunnah prayers at home?
A: Yes. Praying sunnah prayers at home is actually encouraged. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said the best prayer of a person is in his house, except the obligatory prayers, as reported by Al-Bukhari. Praying sunnah at home also brings blessing to the house and teaches children by example. The fard prayers for men are emphasised in congregation.
Q: What is the difference between sunnah and nafl prayers?
A: Sunnah prayers are the voluntary rakats the Prophet, peace be upon him, regularly prayed at fixed times, such as 2 before Fajr. Nafl means any extra voluntary prayer, such as Duha, Tahajjud or additional rakats at any permitted time. All sunnah prayers are a kind of nafl, but the emphasised sunnah prayers carry special status and reward.
:::

Muslims turn to Allah before every important step: an exam, a new job, a journey or a worrying time. The Quran and the Sunnah give us exact words to ask for success, protection, beneficial knowledge and ease. This guide collects the most authentic duas for each, with the Arabic, an easy transliteration, the meaning and the reference, so you and your children can learn them with confidence.

:::key
- **Success:** *Rabbana atina fid dunya hasanah* (Quran 2:201), the dua the Prophet made most.
- **Protection:** *Bismillahil ladhi la yadurru* three times morning and evening.
- **Knowledge:** *Rabbi zidni ilma* (Quran 20:114) and the dua after Fajr for beneficial knowledge.
- **Ease:** *Allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla* and the dua of Musa (Quran 20:25 to 28).
:::

## Dua for Success

True success in Islam means good in this life and the Hereafter. These duas ask for both.

### The most comprehensive dua

Anas said this was the dua the Prophet, peace be upon him, made most often (Sahih al-Bukhari 6389):

:::quote
dua: For good in this world and the next
:::

### Success through Allah's mercy and guidance

The young men of the cave made this dua when they had nowhere else to turn:

:::quote
dua: For mercy and guidance
:::

### Asking for any good you need

Musa, peace be upon him, said this when he was alone, tired and in need in Madyan:

:::quote
dua: For any good you need
:::

## Dua for Protection

The Sunnah gives short, powerful words of protection for every morning and evening.

### Protection from all harm

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said whoever says this three times in the morning and evening, nothing will harm him (Abu Dawud 5088, At-Tirmidhi 3388):

:::quote
dua: For protection from all harm
:::

### Protection from the evil of creation

:::quote
dua: Evening protection
:::

### Ayatul Kursi and the Quls

Reciting Ayatul Kursi before sleep brings a guardian from Allah through the night (Sahih al-Bukhari 2311), and the three Quls morning and evening suffice against everything (Abu Dawud 5082). Read them in full on our page for [Ayatul Kursi and the 4 Quls](/duas-azkar/ayatul-kursi-and-4-quls/), and the whole routine in the [morning and evening azkar](/duas-azkar/morning-evening-azkar/).

## Dua for Knowledge

Knowledge is the only thing Allah told His Prophet to ask for more of.

### Rabbi zidni ilma

:::quote
dua: For more knowledge
:::

### Dua after Fajr for beneficial knowledge

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said this after Fajr (Ibn Majah 925):

:::quote
dua: After Fajr
:::

### Seeking refuge from useless knowledge

He also sought refuge from knowledge that does not benefit and a heart that is not humble (Sahih Muslim 2722). Knowledge is a means to act, not an end in itself.

### The dua of Musa for confidence

Musa, peace be upon him, asked for this before facing Pharaoh. Many students say it before speaking or sitting an exam:

:::quote
ayah: 20:25-28
tr: Rabbishrah li sadri, wa yassir li amri, wahlul uqdatan min lisani, yafqahu qawli.
en: My Lord, expand my chest for me, ease my task for me, and untie the knot from my tongue, so they may understand my speech.

## Quick Reference: Which Dua When?

| Situation | Dua | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Success in this life and the next | Rabbana atina fid dunya hasanah | Quran 2:201 |
| Daily protection | Bismillahil ladhi la yadurru (3 times) | Abu Dawud 5088 |
| At night and in new places | A'udhu bikalimatillahit tammat | Sahih Muslim 2708 |
| More knowledge | Rabbi zidni ilma | Quran 20:114 |
| Before study, after Fajr | Allahumma inni as'aluka ilman nafi'a | Ibn Majah 925 |
| Before an exam or hard task | Allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla | Ibn Hibban |
| Before speaking or presenting | Rabbishrah li sadri | Quran 20:25 to 28 |

### Make your own dua too

Alongside these, ask Allah in your own words for your exact need. Read [what dua is and how to make it](/blog/what-is-dua/) for the etiquette.

### Effort and tawakkul

Dua goes with effort. Study, prepare and then trust Allah with the result. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: tie your camel and trust in Allah (At-Tirmidhi 2517).

## Helping Children Learn These Duas

Children remember duas best when they use them at the right moment.

### One dua at a time

Teach *Rabbi zidni ilma* before homework and the protection dua before school. Say them together until your child says them alone. Our [daily duas for kids](/duas-azkar/daily-duas-for-kids/) page has more.

:::faq
Q: What is the best dua for success?
A: The most comprehensive dua for success is Rabbana atina fid dunya hasanah wa fil akhirati hasanah wa qina adhaban nar, from Surah Al-Baqarah verse 201. Anas reported in Sahih al-Bukhari that this was the dua the Prophet, peace be upon him, made most often. It asks for good in this world, good in the Hereafter and protection from the Fire.
Q: What dua should I read for protection?
A: Say Bismillahil ladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil ardi wa la fis sama'i wa huwas sami'ul alim three times every morning and evening. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said nothing will harm the one who says it, as narrated by Abu Dawud and At-Tirmidhi. Also recite Ayatul Kursi and the three Quls morning, evening and before sleep.
Q: Is there a dua for exams in Islam?
A: There is no dua narrated specifically for exams, but authentic duas for ease and knowledge are ideal. Say Allahumma la sahla illa ma ja'altahu sahla, the dua of Musa Rabbishrah li sadri wa yassir li amri, and Rabbi zidni ilma. Then prepare well and trust Allah. You can also ask in your own words for help with your exam.
Q: What is the dua for knowledge?
A: The best known dua for knowledge is Rabbi zidni ilma, meaning My Lord, increase me in knowledge, from Surah Taha verse 114. After Fajr, the Prophet, peace be upon him, also said Allahumma inni as'aluka ilman nafi'an wa rizqan tayyiban wa amalan mutaqabbala, asking for beneficial knowledge, pure provision and accepted deeds, as narrated by Ibn Majah.
Q: Can I read these duas in English?
A: You can make personal dua in any language, including English or Urdu, and Allah understands every language. However, the duas from the Quran and Sunnah carry their own blessing in Arabic, so it is good to learn the Arabic words with their meaning. Start by saying the Arabic with the transliteration while thinking about the English meaning.
:::

Wudu is the ritual washing a Muslim does before salah, before touching the mushaf and before other acts of worship. It takes about two to three minutes and follows a set order taught by the Prophet, peace be upon him. This guide walks through each step of wudu, explains which parts are obligatory, gives the dua to say afterwards, lists what breaks wudu and shares tips for teaching it to children.

:::key
- The **four obligatory** parts come from Quran 5:6: wash the **face**, wash the **arms to the elbows**, wipe the **head**, wash the **feet to the ankles**.
- The full sunnah wudu adds **Bismillah, hands, mouth, nose and ears**, usually each washed **three times**.
- After wudu, say the **shahadah dua** and the gates of Paradise are opened (Sahih Muslim 234).
- Wudu breaks with **using the toilet, passing wind, deep sleep** and loss of consciousness.
:::

## What Wudu Is and Why It Matters

Wudu (وضوء) means cleanliness and brightness. It purifies the body for prayer and, as the hadith teach, wipes away minor sins.

### The command in the Quran

:::quote
ayah: 5:6
en: O you who believe, when you rise to pray, wash your faces and your hands up to the elbows, wipe over your heads, and wash your feet up to the ankles.
:::

### The reward of wudu

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said that when a Muslim washes his face in wudu, every sin his eyes looked at leaves with the water, and likewise with his hands and feet, until he comes out pure from sins (Sahih Muslim 244). On the Day of Judgement, his followers will be known by the brightness of their wudu (Sahih al-Bukhari 136).

## How to Perform Wudu Step by Step

Follow these steps in order. Use clean water and do not waste it, even at a flowing river.

:::steps
1. **Intention and Bismillah.** Intend wudu in your heart and say *Bismillah*.
2. **Wash the hands** up to the wrists three times, between the fingers too.
3. **Rinse the mouth** three times, swirling water around.
4. **Clean the nose** three times: sniff water in gently with the right hand and blow it out with the left.
5. **Wash the face** three times, from the hairline to the chin and from ear to ear. Men run wet fingers through the beard.
6. **Wash the arms** from the fingertips to and including the elbows, three times, right arm first, then left.
7. **Wipe the head** once with wet hands, from the front to the back and back to the front.
8. **Wipe the ears** once: index fingers inside, thumbs behind.
9. **Wash the feet** up to and including the ankles three times, right foot first, between the toes too.
10. **Say the dua after wudu** (below).
:::

### How long wudu takes

A careful wudu takes about two to three minutes. Rushing causes missed spots, so take your time, especially while learning.

### The dua after wudu

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said whoever performs wudu well and then says this, the eight gates of Paradise are opened for him to enter by whichever he wishes (Sahih Muslim 234):

:::quote
dua: After wudu
:::

## Fard and Sunnah Acts of Wudu

Not every step carries the same weight. Some are obligatory, others are sunnah.

| Act | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Washing the face | Fard | Quran 5:6 |
| Washing arms to the elbows | Fard | Quran 5:6 |
| Wiping the head | Fard | Hanafis: at least a quarter; others differ on how much |
| Washing feet to the ankles | Fard | Quran 5:6 |
| Intention, order and continuity | Fard in some schools, sunnah in others | Shafi'i and Hanbali count these as required |
| Bismillah, hands, mouth, nose, ears | Sunnah | Strongly recommended |
| Three times each | Sunnah | Once each is valid |
| Starting with the right | Sunnah | For arms and feet |

### Why schools differ

All four schools agree on the four parts in the verse. They differ on intention, order and doing it without long gaps. Follow your family's school and your teacher.

### Once, twice or three times

The Prophet, peace be upon him, made wudu washing each part once, twice and three times on different occasions (Sahih al-Bukhari 157 to 159). Three is the most complete.

## What Breaks Wudu?

Once you have wudu, it stays until something breaks it. Then you need to repeat it before the next prayer.

:::cards
- **Using the toilet** | Urine, stool or anything leaving the private parts.
- **Passing wind** | Even without sound or smell, once you are sure.
- **Deep sleep** | Sleep in which you lose awareness, especially lying down.
- **Losing consciousness** | Fainting, or anything that removes awareness.
:::

### Points where scholars differ

Bleeding, vomiting a mouthful, touching the private parts and touching the opposite gender are treated differently by the schools. Ask your teacher which view your family follows.

### When in doubt

If you are sure you had wudu and only doubt whether it broke, you still have wudu. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said not to leave the prayer until you hear a sound or smell something (Sahih al-Bukhari 137).

## Common Mistakes and Practical Tips

These small errors are common, and easy to correct.

### Missing spots

Dry patches on the heels, elbows or between the toes make wudu incomplete. The Prophet, peace be upon him, warned about dry heels (Sahih al-Bukhari 165).

### Nail polish and waterproof layers

Ordinary nail polish stops water reaching the nails, so it must be removed before wudu. Henna is fine because it is only a colour.

### Wiping over socks

You may wipe over leather socks (khuff), and many scholars allow thick socks, if they were put on after a complete wudu: one day and night when at home, three days when travelling (Sahih Muslim 276).

## Teaching Wudu to Children

Children learn wudu by doing it next to a parent, many times.

### Make it a game of order

Sing the steps in order or use a picture chart by the sink. Let them do it while you watch and gently remind them of missed parts.

### Link it to salah

Wudu makes sense to children when it leads to prayer. Teach it together with [how to pray salah](/blog/how-to-pray-salah-step-by-step/). Our [online salah classes](/online-salah-classes/) and [Quran classes for new Muslims](/quran-classes-for-new-muslims/) cover both.

:::faq
Q: What are the steps of wudu in order?
A: Intend wudu and say Bismillah, wash your hands three times, rinse your mouth three times, clean your nose three times, wash your face three times, wash your right then left arm to the elbows three times, wipe your head once, wipe your ears, then wash your right then left foot to the ankles three times. Finish with the shahadah dua.
Q: What are the four fard acts of wudu?
A: The four obligatory acts, from Surah Al-Ma'idah verse 6, are washing the whole face, washing both arms up to and including the elbows, wiping the head, and washing both feet up to and including the ankles. Some schools of thought also make intention, the correct order and doing it without long breaks obligatory. Everything else is sunnah.
Q: What is the dua after wudu?
A: After wudu, say Ash-hadu an la ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh. It means I bear witness that none has the right to be worshipped but Allah alone, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. Sahih Muslim reports that the eight gates of Paradise are opened for whoever says it.
Q: What breaks wudu?
A: All scholars agree that wudu is broken by urine, stool or wind leaving the body, deep sleep in which you lose awareness, and losing consciousness. Some schools add bleeding, vomiting a mouthful, touching the private parts or touching the opposite gender. If you are only unsure whether wudu broke, it remains valid until you are certain.
Q: Can I pray more than one prayer with one wudu?
A: Yes. One wudu lasts until something breaks it, so you can pray several prayers, read the Quran and do other acts of worship with it. It is recommended, though not required, to renew wudu for each prayer. Making a fresh wudu when you still have one brings extra reward and is a sunnah many Muslims keep.
:::

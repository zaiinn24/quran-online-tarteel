Tafseer (also spelled tafsir) is the explanation of the Quran: what its verses mean, why they were revealed, how the Prophet, peace be upon him, and his companions understood them, and what lessons they carry. A translation tells you what a verse says; tafseer helps you understand what it means. This guide explains tafseer simply, lists trusted tafseer books in English and Urdu, and gives you a plan to start, even as a complete beginner.

:::key
- **Tafseer meaning:** explanation and interpretation of the Quran, from the Arabic root *fassara*, to clarify.
- Translation gives the **words**; tafseer gives the **context, meaning and lessons**.
- Trusted starting books include **Tafsir Ibn Kathir (abridged)**, **Maariful Quran** and **Tafheem-ul-Quran**.
- Start with **Al-Fatiha and Juz Amma**, the surahs you recite most, and study with a teacher.
:::

## What Is Tafseer?

Tafseer is a science with rules. It is not personal opinion about what a verse "feels like".

### The sources of tafseer

Scholars explain the Quran in this order of priority: the Quran explaining itself, the Sunnah of the Prophet, peace be upon him, the understanding of the companions, then the Arabic language and the scholars who followed. Every reliable tafseer builds on these.

### Translation versus tafseer

| | Translation | Tafseer |
|---|---|---|
| What it gives | The meaning of the words in another language | Explanation, context, rulings and lessons |
| Length | About the length of the Quran | Many volumes for full works |
| Example | "Guide us to the straight path" | What the straight path is, with verses and hadith that explain it |
| Best use | Daily reading with meaning | Deeper study, ideally with a teacher |

### Why context matters

Some verses were revealed about specific events (asbab an-nuzul). Without context, a verse can be misunderstood. Tafseer protects the reader from this.

## Trusted Tafseer Books to Start With

These tafseers are widely used and respected. Choose one in the language you read best.

:::cards
- **Tafsir Ibn Kathir** | Classical tafseer by Imam Ibn Kathir, explaining verses with other verses, hadith and the sayings of the companions. An abridged English edition is widely available.
- **Maariful Quran** | By Mufti Muhammad Shafi, in Urdu with an English translation. Clear explanations and rulings, popular in South Asia.
- **Tafheem-ul-Quran** | By Maulana Maududi, in Urdu with English translation (Towards Understanding the Quran). Readable and focused on the message.
- **Tafsir as-Sa'di** | A concise classical Arabic tafseer by Shaykh Abdur-Rahman as-Sa'di, available in English. Good for clear, short explanations.
:::

### Online tafseer

[Quran.com](https://quran.com/) offers several tafseers alongside each verse, including Ibn Kathir in English, which makes it easy to look up a verse you are studying.

### Start short, not huge

Full tafseers run to many volumes. Beginners do better with an abridged edition or a teacher who selects the key points.

## A Simple Plan to Start Studying Tafseer

You do not need to start with Surah Al-Baqarah. Start with what you recite every day.

:::steps
1. **Al-Fatiha first.** Learn its meaning word by word and its tafseer. You recite it at least 17 times a day.
2. **The short surahs you pray with,** such as Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas and Al-Asr.
3. **The rest of Juz Amma,** a surah a week.
4. **Surahs with weekly or nightly practice,** like [Surah Al-Kahf](/blog/surah-al-kahf-on-friday/) and [Surah Al-Mulk](/blog/surah-al-mulk-benefits/).
5. **Then longer surahs,** with a teacher or study circle.
:::

### Keep a reflection notebook

Write down one lesson from each passage and one way to act on it. This turns study into change.

## Why Study Tafseer with a Teacher?

Books are valuable, but a teacher brings three things a book cannot.

### Answers to your questions

When a verse raises a question, a teacher can explain it in your context and point you to the right sources.

### Balance and correct understanding

A trained teacher keeps you within the mainstream understanding of the scholars and away from unusual interpretations found online.

### Linking meaning and recitation

Our [Translation and Tafseer classes](/quran-translation-tafseer-classes/) combine word by word meaning with tafseer, so every surah you recite in salah comes alive. Sisters can study in our [Quran classes for women](/online-quran-classes-for-women/).

## Tafseer for Children

Children can learn tafseer too, in a simpler form.

### Stories and simple lessons

Start with the stories of the Prophets and the meaning of short surahs. See our [Islamic stories for kids](/blog/islamic-stories-for-kids/).

### Age-appropriate depth

For children, focus on the main message and one action: Surah Al-Asr teaches us not to waste time, Surah Al-Ma'un teaches us to help others. Our [Islamic studies online](/online-islamic-studies/) classes teach this way.

Q: What does tafseer mean?
A: Tafseer means the explanation and interpretation of the Quran. The word comes from the Arabic root fassara, meaning to clarify or uncover. A tafseer explains what verses mean, the context in which they were revealed, how the Prophet, peace be upon him, and his companions understood them, and the lessons and rulings they contain. It goes far beyond a translation.
Q: Which tafseer is best for beginners?
A: Many beginners start with an abridged English edition of Tafsir Ibn Kathir, or Maariful Quran by Mufti Muhammad Shafi, which is available in Urdu and English. Tafheem-ul-Quran and Tafsir as-Sa'di are also widely read. Choose one in the language you read best, start with Al-Fatiha and Juz Amma, and study with a teacher if you can.
Q: What is the difference between tafseer and translation?
A: A translation conveys the meaning of the Quran's words in another language, about the same length as the Quran. A tafseer explains those words in depth, giving context, related verses and hadith, the understanding of the companions, rulings and lessons. You can read a translation daily, while tafseer is for deeper study of each passage.
Q: Can I study tafseer on my own?
A: You can read reliable tafseer books on your own and benefit greatly, especially for reflection. However, studying with a qualified teacher helps you understand difficult passages correctly, answers your questions and keeps you away from unusual interpretations. A good approach is to read a trusted tafseer yourself and attend regular classes to discuss what you read.
Q: What is Tafsir Ibn Kathir?
A: Tafsir Ibn Kathir is a classical explanation of the Quran by Imam Ismail ibn Kathir, a scholar of the 8th Islamic century. It explains verses using other verses of the Quran, authentic hadith and the sayings of the companions and early scholars. It is one of the most widely read tafseers, and abridged English editions make it accessible to beginners.
:::

Surah Al-Mulk (The Sovereignty) is the 67th surah of the Quran, with 30 verses, and opens the 29th juz. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said it intercedes for its reader until he is forgiven, and he would not sleep until he had recited it. Many Muslims recite it every night before sleeping. This guide explains the authentic benefits of Surah Al-Mulk, its meaning and themes, and a simple plan to memorise it.

:::key
- **30 verses**, a Makki surah, the first surah of **Juz 29** (Tabarak).
- It **intercedes for its reader until he is forgiven** (At-Tirmidhi 2891, Abu Dawud 1400).
- The Prophet, peace be upon him, **did not sleep until he recited** As-Sajdah and Al-Mulk (At-Tirmidhi 2892).
- Recited every night, it is described as **protection from the punishment of the grave**.
:::

## The Benefits of Surah Al-Mulk in Authentic Hadith

The virtues of Surah Al-Mulk come from hadith that scholars have graded reliable.

### It intercedes until its reader is forgiven

:::quote
en: There is a surah in the Quran of thirty verses which interceded for a man until he was forgiven. It is: Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion.
ref: At-Tirmidhi 2891, Abu Dawud 1400, graded hasan
:::

### Protection from the punishment of the grave

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud said that whoever recites *Tabarak alladhi biyadihil mulk* every night, Allah will protect him by it from the punishment of the grave, and that it was called *al-mani'ah*, the protector (An-Nasa'i in As-Sunan al-Kubra, graded hasan by Al-Albani).

### The Prophet's nightly practice

Jabir reported that the Prophet, peace be upon him, would not sleep until he recited *Alif Lam Mim Tanzil* (Surah As-Sajdah) and *Tabarak alladhi biyadihil mulk* (At-Tirmidhi 2892). This is the basis for reciting Al-Mulk at night. Add it to your [duas before sleeping](/duas-azkar/sleeping-duas/).

## What Surah Al-Mulk Is About

The surah begins by declaring Allah's complete control over everything:

:::quote
ayah: 67:1-2
en: Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion, and He is over all things competent. The One who created death and life to test you, which of you is best in deeds, and He is the Almighty, the Forgiving.
:::

### Main themes

| Verses | Theme |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | Allah's sovereignty, life and death as a test, the perfect creation of the heavens |
| 6 to 11 | The regret of those who denied the warning |
| 12 to 14 | Reward for those who fear Allah unseen; Allah knows what is in hearts |
| 15 to 22 | The earth made easy for us, birds held in the sky, provision from Allah alone |
| 23 to 30 | Hearing, sight and hearts as gifts; who could bring water if it sank away? |

### Lessons for daily life

Life is a test of who does the best deeds, not the most. Everything we rely on, from the ground we walk on to the water we drink, is held by Allah. The surah ends with a question that leaves the reader humble.

## When and How to Recite Surah Al-Mulk

There is no fixed time required, but the Sunnah points to the night.

### Every night before sleep

Following the hadith of Jabir, recite it after Isha or in bed. Many families make it part of the bedtime routine with their children.

### Recite with Tajweed

Al-Mulk has many examples of madd, ghunnah and qalqalah, which makes it a good practice surah. See [what Tajweed is](/blog/what-is-tajweed/) or improve with our [online Tajweed classes](/online-tajweed-classes/).

### Listen if you cannot read yet

If you are still learning to read, listen to it nightly with a translation while you learn. Listening is the first step to memorising.

## How to Memorize Surah Al-Mulk in Two Weeks

Thirty verses sounds like a lot, but split into small portions it takes two to three weeks.

:::steps
1. **Days 1 to 3:** verses 1 to 6. Listen ten times, then repeat each verse aloud until fluent.
2. **Days 4 to 6:** verses 7 to 12, then recite 1 to 12 together.
3. **Days 7 to 9:** verses 13 to 19, then recite 1 to 19.
4. **Days 10 to 12:** verses 20 to 25, then recite 1 to 25.
5. **Days 13 to 14:** verses 26 to 30, then the whole surah, twice a day.
6. **Ongoing:** recite it every night. That nightly recitation is your revision.
:::

### Tips that help

Use the same mushaf every day, recite new verses in your sunnah prayers and recite to a teacher before moving on. Read [how to memorise the Quran](/blog/how-to-memorize-quran/) for more techniques.

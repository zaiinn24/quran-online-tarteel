Laylatul Qadr, the Night of Decree or Night of Power, is the night the Quran was first revealed, and worship in it is better than a thousand months. It falls in the last ten nights of Ramadan, most likely on an odd night. In 2027, with Ramadan expected to begin around 8 February, the last ten nights are expected to begin around the evening of 27 February, depending on the moon sighting. This guide covers the dua for Laylatul Qadr, its signs and a simple plan for each night.

:::key
- Laylatul Qadr is **better than a thousand months** (Quran 97:3).
- Seek it in the **odd nights of the last ten**: the 21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th and 29th (Sahih al-Bukhari 2017).
- The Prophet's dua for this night: **Allahumma innaka afuwwun tuhibbul afwa fa'fu anni** (At-Tirmidhi 3513).
- Worship **every one of the last ten nights**, so you do not miss it.
:::

## What Is Laylatul Qadr?

Allah revealed a whole surah about this night:

:::quote
ayah: 97:1-3
en: Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree. And what will make you know what the Night of Decree is? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months.
:::

### Better than a thousand months

A thousand months is more than 83 years, a whole lifetime. Worship on this one night is better than worship for all of that time.

### Forgiveness for the past

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Whoever stands in prayer on Laylatul Qadr out of faith and hoping for reward, his previous sins will be forgiven" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1901).

## When Is Laylatul Qadr 2027?

The exact night is hidden, so that believers strive in all the last ten nights.

### The odd nights

"Seek Laylatul Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramadan" (Sahih al-Bukhari 2017). Islamic nights begin at sunset, so the 21st night starts at Maghrib after the 20th fast.

| Night | Expected evening in 2027 (if Ramadan begins 8 February) |
|---|---|
| 21st | Saturday 27 February |
| 23rd | Monday 1 March |
| 25th | Wednesday 3 March |
| 27th | Friday 5 March |
| 29th | Sunday 7 March |

These dates are estimates. Confirm the start of Ramadan with your local mosque, then count from there.

### The 27th night

Many Muslims give special attention to the 27th night, based on reports from some companions. Scholars still advise worshipping all ten nights, because the Prophet, peace be upon him, did not fix one date.

## The Dua for Laylatul Qadr

Aishah asked the Prophet, peace be upon him, what she should say if she knew which night was Laylatul Qadr. He taught her:

:::quote
dua: In the last ten nights (Laylatul Qadr)
:::

### Say it often

Repeat it throughout the night, in sujood, between prayers and before suhoor. Add your own duas in any language. Read [what dua is and how to make it](/blog/what-is-dua/) for the etiquette.

## How the Prophet Spent the Last Ten Nights

Aishah said that when the last ten nights began, the Prophet, peace be upon him, would tighten his waist wrapper, stay up the whole night and wake his family (Sahih al-Bukhari 2024).

### I'tikaf

He would also spend the last ten days in i'tikaf, staying in the mosque for worship (Sahih al-Bukhari 2026). Women can observe i'tikaf too, and many sisters set aside a quiet prayer space at home for these nights.

### Waking the family

Involve your children: let older ones stay up for part of the night, pray two rakats with you and make dua. See [how to prepare kids for Ramadan](/blog/prepare-kids-for-ramadan/).

## A Simple Plan for Each of the Last Ten Nights

You do not need to stay up all night to benefit. Choose a plan you can keep for all ten nights.

:::steps
1. **After Maghrib:** break your fast, pray Maghrib and make dua before eating much.
2. **Isha and Taraweeh:** pray in congregation or at home.
3. **Quran:** read your daily portion from the [Ramadan Quran plan](/blog/ramadan-quran-plan/), or Surah Al-Qadr with its meaning.
4. **Dhikr:** SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar and La ilaha illallah, as in [what dhikr is](/blog/what-is-dhikr/).
5. **Last third of the night:** two or more rakats of Tahajjud and long sujood with dua.
6. **Before Fajr:** suhoor, istighfar and the Laylatul Qadr dua.
:::

### Charity

Give something every night of the last ten, even a small amount online. If one of those nights is Laylatul Qadr, it is charity on that night.

### For sisters who are not praying

Women who are menstruating can still make dua, dhikr, istighfar, listen to the Quran and give charity, and many scholars allow reciting from memory. You can still gain the blessings of the night.

## Signs of Laylatul Qadr

Some signs are mentioned in authentic hadith.

### The morning sun

Ubayy ibn Ka'b reported that on the morning after Laylatul Qadr, the sun rises without strong rays (Sahih Muslim 762).

### Do not chase signs

Signs are only noticed afterwards. The goal is not to find the night but to worship sincerely in all of them.

Q: What is the dua for Laylatul Qadr?
A: The dua the Prophet, peace be upon him, taught Aishah for Laylatul Qadr is Allahumma innaka afuwwun karimun tuhibbul afwa fa'fu anni, meaning O Allah, You are Most Forgiving and Generous, You love to forgive, so forgive me. It is narrated by At-Tirmidhi. Repeat it often in all the last ten nights, especially in sujood and before suhoor.
Q: When is Laylatul Qadr in 2027?
A: Laylatul Qadr falls in the last ten nights of Ramadan, most likely on an odd night. If Ramadan 2027 begins around 8 February, the 21st night begins around the evening of 27 February, and the 27th night around the evening of 5 March. Dates depend on the local moon sighting, so confirm with your mosque.
Q: What should I do on Laylatul Qadr?
A: Pray Isha and Taraweeh, recite the Quran, make plenty of dhikr and istighfar, pray Tahajjud in the last third of the night and repeat the Laylatul Qadr dua. Give some charity each night. Whoever prays on this night out of faith and hoping for reward will have previous sins forgiven, as narrated by Al-Bukhari.
Q: Why is Laylatul Qadr better than a thousand months?
A: Allah says in Surah Al-Qadr that the Night of Decree is better than a thousand months, which is more than 83 years. It is the night the Quran was first revealed, the angels and Jibreel descend, and it is peace until dawn. Worship on this single night is rewarded more than a lifetime of worship.
Q: Can a woman on her period benefit from Laylatul Qadr?
A: Yes. A woman who is menstruating does not pray salah, but she can make dua, dhikr, istighfar and salawat, listen to the Quran, give charity and reflect on the meaning of the Quran. Many scholars also allow her to recite the Quran from memory. She can still worship and seek the blessings of the night.
:::

Our classes

Quran Online Tarteel offers live one-to-one online Quran and Islamic studies classes for children under 18 and adult women, taught by female teachers.

Free trial

New students may take 2 free trial classes. No payment details are required for the trial.

Scheduling

Class days and times are agreed with you. Please give reasonable notice if you need to reschedule.

Fees and payment

After the trial, classes are billed monthly according to the plan you choose. Current fees are shared on request.

Conduct

Students and teachers are expected to behave respectfully. For children, a parent or guardian is responsible for the child's attendance and setup.

Website content

Guides on this website are for general learning. For religious rulings, please consult qualified scholars.

Contact
